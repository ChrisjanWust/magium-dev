# Unit B2-U2 — Book 2, Chapter 3

## Unit focus

A single-file unit and the smallest in Book 2 (~17k words, 48 scenes, no route branches). Use the
headroom for **depth rather than breadth**: this is the unit where you can afford full paragraph-level
rewrites rather than surgical patches.

`b2ch3` **reads** `v_b2_ch2_deal` (set upstream in ch2/ch4b/ch9b) but sets almost nothing that
travels. That makes it low-risk for cross-file damage and a good candidate for the most aggressive
prose work in the book.

**Primary targets:**

- **P7 — white-room staging.** With no branch variants to sweep, every scene is a single pass. Go
  through all 48 and ask of each: could this be performed on a bare stage? Anchor conversation in
  camp chores, weather, terrain, injury, food, weapon maintenance.
- **P6 — pacing.** Check for reveals or deaths that get less than a paragraph of aftermath. Add
  runways (pre-reveal stillness, hesitation) and landing pads (sensory aftermath, physical recovery).
- **P1 — the underdog paradox.** Any combat or problem-solving beat here should resolve through
  Barry reading a mechanical constraint, not through a stat number being large. If the device
  carries a heavy load, show the physical cost on a human frame.

## Secondary

- **P2** — chapter 3 is early enough that party friction from Book 1 should still be live. Do not
  let the group settle into comfortable banter unless a specific earned moment has bought it.
