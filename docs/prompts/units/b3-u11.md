# Unit B3-U11 — Book 3, Chapters 11–12 (the finale)

## Unit focus

**This is the ending of the trilogy** — chapter 11 plus both routes of chapter 12, ~46.2k words.
Everything three books have built resolves in your files. Read
`docs/characters/_segments/16-b3-ch11-12.md` in full before touching anything.

### Primary target — P6, event density. This is the failure mode most likely to recur here.

Book 1's Chapter 7 was the project's worst structural failure: eight major beats stacked back to
back — knifepoint standoff, orphanage reveal, character introduction, duel, a man incinerated, sage
debate, dragon duel, cliffhanger — with the emotional climax arriving after the reader was already
exhausted. `docs/fix10-ch7-pacing-plan.md` is the complete worked analysis of that failure and its
remedy. **Read it before you start.** It documents precisely what a runway and a landing pad look like
and where to insert them.

A trilogy finale is structurally identical to that trap: multiple threads demanding resolution,
several deaths or partings, a climactic confrontation, and a denouement — all competing for the same
few thousand words. `b3ch12b` is 18.9k words with **91 choices across 46 scenes**, the densest file in
your unit.

Audit for **five endings stacked**. Every death, reveal, reunion, sacrifice, and victory needs
pre-beat stillness and post-beat sensory aftermath. The emotional climax must land while the reader
still has capacity for it.

### Primary target — P4, deus ex machina. Highest stakes in the project.

A finale with the full ensemble plus Eiden, Arraka, stillwaters, and whatever the climax escalates to
is maximum bailout risk. **The party must solve the ending.** If an overpowered ally resolves the
central confrontation, three books of stakes evaporate on the last page.

Reference fix — committed Book 1 text, read it: `data/en/ch6.magium`, scenes `Ch6-Win-fight` and
`Ch6-Eiden-leaves`. Eiden was removed from the win path entirely; the ancient trap fires from
**environmental causation** (accumulated heat from the party's own fight); Eiden is reduced to a
disembodied *"Well done, Barry"* from an empty sky. The party kept its victory, Barry paid for it
physically, and the antagonist kept his menace.

### Secondary — P3 residue

`b3ch12b` runs **1.98 choices per scene** — a sweep S1 priority target. For every label S1 replaced,
verify in the target scene that the echo line repeats it verbatim, the NPC reaction still lands, and
the reaction does not reference a joke that no longer exists.

  **Book 2 seam flag**. You cannot edit Book 2. If the inbound assumption looks wrong, report it as

- `b3ch12a` reads `v_ch11_saved_rose` (Book 1)
- `b3ch12b` reads `v_ch11_saved_rose` (Book 1)

Both chapter 12 routes reading a Book 1 flag means **the trilogy's final pages depend on a decision
made in Book 1 chapter 11.** Read `data/en/ch11a.magium` and `ch11b.magium` so you know what you are
paying off. Getting this wrong is the most visible possible failure in the project.

### Cast canon at the finale

Every character must exit as themselves. **Kate**'s concern still presents as irritation; grief and
fear still present as coldness — do not let the finale melt her into open sentiment she has never
shown. **Daren** talks like a legend and then undercuts himself. **Leila is mute** — glowing midair
letters only. **Flower and Illuna are two people.** **Arraka** stays cruel even when helping.
**Hadrik**'s drinking is joy and ritual, never trauma medication, and he does not finish the helpless.
**Barry** remains an ordinary human with no innate magic — if he wins the trilogy on a stat number
rather than a loophole, Pattern 1 has beaten us on the last page.
