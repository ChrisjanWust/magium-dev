# Unit B3-U8 — Book 3, Chapter 8 (both routes)

## Unit focus

Both routes of chapter 8 (~26.2k words), and **the most lopsided decision split in the book.**

`b3ch8a` is 9.4k words / 22 scenes and **sets nothing** — it reads `v_b3_ch4_billy`,
`v_b3_ch8_king`, `v_b3_ch8_overseer2`.

`b3ch8b` is 16.8k words / 30 scenes and **sets six flags**: `v_b3_ch8_king`, `v_b3_ch8_overseer2`,
`v_b3_ch6_kelrim`, `v_b3_ch6_plan`, `v_b3_ch6_labels`, `v_b3_ch4_billy`.

So `8b` is where the late-book plot is decided and `8a` is where it is absorbed. **That asymmetry is
the Pattern 5 risk: an absorption route is exactly where writers default to neutral prose.** Whatever
the player did must change the emotional register in `8a`, not merely the routing through it.

`v_b3_ch8_overseer2` is read in `b3ch9b` and `b3ch9c`; `v_b3_ch8_king` is read in `b3ch9a` and
`b3ch10a`. Both feed the convergence sinks. Record precisely what each value means emotionally in your
`CONTINUITY EXIT STATE` — units B3-U9 and B3-U10 depend on it.

**Primary target — P7, white-room dialogue.** `b3ch8b` averages **~561 prose words per scene**, among
the highest in the project. Long scenes with a king and an overseer in them will be negotiation and
exposition — the exact conditions for floating-head dialogue. With 52 scenes across both files you can
work every one.

Ask of each: could this be performed on a bare stage? If yes, stage it. Interleave physical business
**through** the dialogue rather than appending scenery. Calibration passages: Master Contract §7.

**Secondary — P4, deus ex machina.** Late book with a king, an overseer, and the full ensemble on
stage. Audit every scene where an overpowered ally could trivially end the threat. Reference fix:
`data/en/ch6.magium`, scenes `Ch6-Win-fight` and `Ch6-Eiden-leaves` — the bailout became environmental
causation plus disembodied menace and the party kept its victory.

**Arraka canon check:** if she is explaining lore helpfully and neutrally anywhere here, that is a
break. Help arrives as insult, delayed reveal, or a joke timed to wound.
