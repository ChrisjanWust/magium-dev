## Split notice — `b3ch10a` and `b3ch10b` are NOT yours

They belong to **B3-U10**, running concurrently.

The risk runs both ways: **a prose block you rewrite here may exist in near-identical form in `10a` or
`10b`.** You cannot see B3-U10's edits and must not make them yourself.

**Your obligation:** whenever you rewrite a passage you have reason to believe also appears in a
sibling route, record it under `CROSS-FILE` in this form:



a miss leaves a visible inconsistency on a live route.
