# Unit B1-U4 — Book 1, Chapter 6

## Mode: deepen — the reference chapter, unevenly finished

`ch6` is the second-largest file in Book 1 (31,007 words, 72 scenes, 141 choices) and received 11% of
its prose lines rewritten. **The parts that were done are the project's reference implementations. The
rest of the chapter was not touched.**

### What was done — do not undo

**The dragon win path (Pattern 4 reference).** Eiden was removed from all three `Ch6-Win-fight`
variants. The lessathi trap now fires **environmentally** from the accumulated heat of the party's own
fight. Barry collapses with a nosebleed and shaking hands as the device drains. Kate delivers the
exposition. Eiden is reduced to a disembodied *"Well done, Barry"* from an empty sky. `Ch6-Win-fight2`
was rewritten so Kate explains the trap instead of Eiden, and a new win-path variant of
`Ch6-Eiden-leaves` was added.

**Note on that last change:** two `#if` blocks were added to `Ch6-Eiden-leaves`, taking its paragraph
entries from 1 to 4. That was a deliberate authorial change made before the current rules existed. It is
committed, it is now the baseline, and **you must not revert it** — nor do anything similar. Writers are
forbidden from touching `#if` structure (Master Contract §3).

**The Rose flirt sequence (Pattern 3/7 reference).** The stream-bank setup was restaged with physical
proximity — shared cold water, a bared forearm, neither pulling back. Five choice labels were replaced
with survival-calculus humour across `Ch6-Travel`, `Ch6-Flirt`, and `Ch6-Passion`, with their echoes and
NPC reactions updated.

### What was NOT done

- **The dragon fight itself.** Tyrath's combat is the chapter's centrepiece and only its *resolution*
  was rewritten. Audit the exchange for Pattern 1 (does Barry contribute analysis, or just numbers?) and
  Pattern 6 (does a dragon fight get aftermath, or cut straight to conversation?).
- **`Ch6-Lose-fight` and `Ch6-Lose-fight2`.** On the losing path Eiden *does* intervene, and that is
  narratively correct — but it was never audited for how the asymmetry reads. Compare against the win
  path's treatment.
- **`Ch6-Backstory`** and the travel/fruit-picking stretch — long dialogue, minimal staging.
- **The `Ch6-Ashamed` / `Ch6-Simple` / `Ch6-Freeze` branch** after Eiden leaves, where Rose scolds Daren.
- **Roughly 60 of 72 scenes.**
