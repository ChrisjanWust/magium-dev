# Unit B2-U1 — Book 2, Chapters 1–2

## Unit focus

This is the **opening of Book 2**, which makes it the highest-leverage continuity unit in the book.
Everything downstream inherits the emotional temperature you set here.

**Your primary job is Pattern 2 and Pattern 5**, and they are entangled:

`b2ch1` **sets** three inherited Book 1 flags — `v_ch11_saved_rose`, `v_ch11_saved_slave`,
`v_ch11_romance_rose` — and `b2ch2` immediately **reads** all three. This is the seam where Book 1's
outcomes become Book 2's reality.

Before you edit a single line, read Book 1's endings so you know what you are inheriting:

- `data/en/ch11a.magium` — the romance/rescue route (read the `Ch11a-Racing2` and
  `Ch11a-Feelings2` scenes; `v_ch11_romance_rose == 1` means Barry and Rose became physically
  intimate)
- `data/en/ch11b.magium` — the alternate route

**On the `v_ch11_romance_rose == 1` route, Barry and Rose must not read as strangers. On the `0`
route, prose must not imply intimacy that never happened.** Check every Rose beat in both files
against both route values. This exact failure is what Pattern 5 exists to catch.

`b2ch1` also sets `v_b2_ch1_interfere` (read later in ch6, ch7, ch8) and `b2ch1` sets
`v_b2_ch1_puzzle2` (read in ch9a). Report both as `CROSS-FILE` if your edits change what they imply.

## Secondary targets

- **P2** — Book 1 closed with a fragile, hard-won party. Verify chapter 1 does not open with
  everyone comfortable. Whatever friction Book 1 earned should still be visible in body language
  and speech register.
- **P7** — `b2ch2` averages ~327 prose words per scene, the lowest in your unit. Short scenes are
  often the ones stripped of terrain and physical business. Stage them.
- **P8** — you are the opening chapters; register-setting matters most here.

## Do not

- Do not edit `data/en/ch11a.magium` or `ch11b.magium`. Read-only. They are committed Book 1.
- Do not change any `choice(...)` label. Sweep S1 has already run.
- Do not invent what happened between books. If the gap needs a fact you do not have, work around it.
