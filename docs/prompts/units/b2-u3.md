# Unit B2-U3 — Book 2, Chapter 4 (both routes)

## Unit focus

Your two files are **the two routes of the same chapter**. That is why they are assigned together:
they almost certainly share near-identical prose blocks, and the single worst failure mode is
fixing a passage in `4a` and leaving its twin stale in `4b`.

**Method: diff the two routes before you edit anything.**

```sh
diff <(grep '^ID: ' data/en/b2ch4a.magium) <(grep '^ID: ' data/en/b2ch4b.magium)
```

Where scene IDs or prose overlap, decide deliberately whether the routes *should* read identically.
Usually they should not — that is Pattern 5.

**Primary target — P5, branch flattening.** `b2ch4b` is the heavier route (19.0k words vs 14.1k,
88 choices vs 63) and it **sets** three flags that travel: `v_b2_ch2_deal`, `v_b2_ch2_lessathi`,
`v_b2_ch2_creatures`, plus `v_ch11_saved_rose`. `b2ch4a` **reads** `v_b2_ch2_deal`,
`v_b2_ch2_lessathi`, `v_ch11_saved_rose` and sets little.

So the asymmetry is real: `4b` is where decisions are made, `4a` is where they are absorbed. Make
sure absorbed decisions actually change the emotional register in `4a`, not just the routing.

**Secondary — P3 residue.** `b2ch4b` has 1.87 choices per scene, above book average. Sweep S1 has
already replaced sanctioned clown labels, but check that the **echo lines** and the surrounding NPC
reactions now match the replaced labels in tone. A dark, analytical Barry line followed by an NPC
reacting to a slapstick joke is a leftover seam.

**Secondary — P2.** Chapter 4 is where mid-book comfort usually creeps in. Verify any thaw in a
volatile relationship was actually paid for by an on-page act.

## Do not

- Do not change any `choice(...)` label. Sweep S1 has already run. If a label still reads as a clown
  line, report it under `UNRESOLVED` rather than editing it.
- Do not edit `b2ch2`, `b2ch5*`, `b2ch7`, or `b2ch9b` — report flag inconsistencies as `CROSS-FILE`.
