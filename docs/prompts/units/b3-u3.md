**Primary target — P5, and this unit carries a specific high-stakes case.**

point** late in the trilogy. On the `== 1` route Barry and Rose became physically intimate at the end
of Book 1 (see `data/en/ch11a.magium`, scenes `Ch11a-Racing2` and `Ch11a-Feelings2`, which are
committed Book 1 text — read them). On `== 0` they did not.

Both inbound states must read correctly, and the state you write out is read downstream in `b3ch10a`
and `b3ch10b`. Getting Rose's register wrong here corrupts the finale. This is the single most
consequential Pattern 5 surface in your unit — treat it as primary work, not a check.

**Rose canon reminder:** mid-twenties herbalist and local guide, **not a damsel**. Competent in her own
domain, naive about the wider magical world. Warm by default, decisive once she chooses. Her
established motivation is a bedridden father whose sickroom shares a wall with a quarantine ward. If
she is reduced to being rescued or to reacting, that is a canon break.
