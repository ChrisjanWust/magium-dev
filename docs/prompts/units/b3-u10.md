# Unit B3-U10 — Book 3, Chapter 10 (routes a and b)

## Unit focus

Two of chapter 10's three routes, ~42k words. **`b3ch10c` is owned by unit B3-U12, not by you.**
Coordinate with it through `CROSS-FILE` reports — see the split notice at the bottom of this section.

### `b3ch10b` — the project's most extreme white-room risk

**`b3ch10b` averages ~723 prose words per scene** (21.7k words / 30 scenes) — the highest density in
the entire project by a wide margin. `b3ch10a` is second at ~580.

**Pattern 7 is your primary assignment.** Scenes this long are almost always extended debate and
exposition with no terrain, no weather, no objects, no bodies. With 65 scenes across both files you
can work every one.

For each scene: **could this be performed on a bare stage?** If yes, stage it. Anchor conversation in
physical business — camp chores, weapon maintenance, injury, food, weather, the feel of ground
underfoot, posture, hands. **Interleave the physical through the dialogue** so action carries subtext.
Do not append scenery paragraphs; that leaves the floating heads intact with wallpaper behind them.

Calibration passages: Master Contract §7. Both are conversations that became scenes because the
characters were doing something with their hands while talking.

### `b3ch10a` — the second convergence sink

`b3ch10a` reads **eight upstream flags** and carries **100 `#if` blocks against 35 scenes** — the
densest conditional structure in the project. Read the generated inbound-flag table above before you
touch it.

Of those, `v_ch11_romance_rose` traces back to the end of **Book 1** and was re-set in `b3ch3a`. On
the `== 1` route Barry and Rose became physically intimate; on `== 0` they did not. It is read in both
your files. This is the finale approach — getting Rose's register wrong here is the most visible
possible continuity failure in the trilogy. Check every Rose beat against both values.

**Pattern 5 at a convergence sink:** eight flags landing in one file is where the original text will
have written one neutral scene and gated only the routing. Prioritise the widest-reach flags
(`v_b3_ch4_billy`, `v_b3_ch6_kelrim`) and any flag representing a loyalty or moral choice over a
tactical one.

**With 100 conditional blocks in `b3ch10a`, the prohibition on adding or removing `#if` blocks is
your sharpest hazard.** Pattern 5 work here means rewriting prose *inside* existing variants so they
diverge emotionally — never introducing a new variant. See Master Contract §3.

### Secondary — P6, pacing

This is the chapter before the finale. Book 1's failure was five endings stacked with the emotional
climax arriving after reader exhaustion — `docs/fix10-ch7-pacing-plan.md` documents it fully.
Every death, reveal, and reversal needs a runway before and a landing pad after.

Report every multi-variant scene under `VARIANTS SWEPT` with the count updated.

## Split notice — `b3ch10c` is NOT yours

Chapter 10's three routes were deliberately split across two units for context-budget reasons.
`b3ch10c` belongs to **B3-U12**, running concurrently with you.

This creates one specific risk: **a prose block you rewrite in `10a`/`10b` may exist in near-identical
form in `10c`.** You cannot see B3-U12's edits and must not make them yourself.

**Your obligation:** whenever you rewrite a passage that you have reason to believe also appears in
`b3ch10c` — recurring descriptions, shared scene beats, repeated character business — record it under
`CROSS-FILE` in this form:

```
- b3ch10c :: <scene id or quoted opening phrase> — I rewrote the twin of this passage in
  b3ch10a/b as: "<one-line summary of the new direction>". Needs matching treatment.
```

To find the candidates cheaply:

```sh
diff <(grep '^ID: ' data/en/b3ch10a.magium) <(grep '^ID: ' data/en/b3ch10c.magium)
diff <(grep '^ID: ' data/en/b3ch10b.magium) <(grep '^ID: ' data/en/b3ch10c.magium)
```

The book-master reconciles these after both units return. Be generous with these reports — a false
positive costs the master one lookup; a miss leaves a visible inconsistency on a live route.

## Do not

- **Do not edit `b3ch10c`.** It is B3-U12's file. Report as `CROSS-FILE`.
- Do not add, remove, or re-nest any `#if` block. Machine-checked; see Master Contract §3.
- Do not change any `choice(...)` label. Sweep S1 has already run.
- Do not edit `b3ch3a`, `b3ch4*`, `b3ch6*`, `b3ch8*`, `b3ch9*`, `ch11a`, or `ch11b`.
- Do not pad. Staging adds words; cutting over-explanation removes them. Net drift ±10% per file.
