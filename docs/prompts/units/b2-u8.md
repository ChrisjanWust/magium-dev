# Unit B2-U8 — Book 2, Chapter 11 (all three routes)

## Unit focus

**This is Book 2's finale, across three route files (~43k words).** It is the largest unit in the
book and the one where Book 1's worst structural failure is most likely to recur.

**Primary target — P6, event density.** The Book 1 lesson: Chapter 7 stacked eight major beats back
to back — knifepoint standoff, orphanage reveal, character introduction, duel, a man incinerated,
sage debate, dragon duel, cliffhanger — and the emotional climax arrived after the reader was already
exhausted. `docs/fix10-ch7-pacing-plan.md` is the full worked analysis of that failure; **read it
before you start.** It documents exactly what a runway and a landing pad look like and where to
insert them.

Book 2's ending has three routes to resolve plus whatever hands off to Book 3. Audit for five endings
stacked. Every death, reveal, reunion, and victory needs pre-beat stillness and post-beat sensory
aftermath.

**Primary target — P4, deus ex machina.** Finales are where an overpowered ally most often resolves
the plot for the party. Reference fix: `data/en/ch6.magium`, scenes `Ch6-Win-fight` and
`Ch6-Eiden-leaves`. Convert bailouts into environmental causation plus disembodied menace. The party
keeps its victory; the antagonist keeps his threat.

**Critical — `v_second_book_purchased` is set in `b2ch11c`.** This is the commercial content gate for
the whole book, read in ten other files. Treat every `#if` keyed on it as structurally sacred: never
merge, reorder, or collapse those branches. Prose in both the purchased and unpurchased variants must
stand independently coherent. Report every edit you make near it.

**Three-way variant sweeping.** With three routes there will be near-identical prose blocks in
triplicate. Fixing one and leaving two stale is the signature bug of this structure:

```sh
for f in a b c; do echo "== 11$f"; grep -c '^ID: ' data/en/b2ch11$f.magium; done
diff <(grep '^ID: ' data/en/b2ch11a.magium) <(grep '^ID: ' data/en/b2ch11b.magium)
```

Report every multi-variant scene under `VARIANTS SWEPT` with the count you updated.

**Secondary — P7.** `b2ch11b` averages ~530 prose words per scene — the longest in Book 2. Stage it.

**Your `CONTINUITY EXIT STATE` report matters more than any other unit's.** Book 3 inherits directly
from your files. Be precise about where every character stands physically and emotionally, and record
the state separately per route where routes diverge.

## Do not

- Do not change any `choice(...)` label. Sweep S1 has already run.
- Do not touch any `b3ch*` file. Book 3 is another master's scope.
- Do not invent the Book 3 opening. End where the text ends.
