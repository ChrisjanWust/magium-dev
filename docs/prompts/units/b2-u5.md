# Unit B2-U5 — Book 2, Chapters 6–7

## Unit focus

Two single-route chapters in the middle of the book. `b2ch6` has the **lowest choice density in
Book 2** (47 choices / 36 scenes = 1.31) — it is a prose-heavy, low-branching stretch, which usually
means extended dialogue and exposition. `b2ch7` is denser (84 / 53).

**Primary target — P4, deus ex machina.** Mid-book is where an overpowered ally is most tempting as
a plot solvent. Audit every scene in both files where Eiden or Arraka is present during a fight the
party is losing, or where a problem resolves without the party solving it.

The reference fix is committed Book 1 text — read it before you start:
`data/en/ch6.magium`, scenes `Ch6-Win-fight` and `Ch6-Eiden-leaves`. The bailout was converted into
**environmental causation** (accumulated heat triggering an ancient trap) plus **disembodied menace**
(Eiden reduced to a voice from an empty sky). The party kept its victory; the antagonist kept his
threat. Apply that shape.

**Arraka-specific canon check:** if Arraka is explaining lore helpfully and neutrally anywhere in
these two files, that is a canon break. Her help always arrives wrapped in an insult, a delayed
reveal, or a joke timed to wound. She may monologue during violence because she is usually in no
danger.

**Secondary — P2.** Both files **read** `v_b2_ch1_interfere` (set back in ch1/ch2) and
`v_ch11_saved_rose`; `b2ch7` also reads `v_b2_ch2_deal`, `v_ch11_saved_slave`, `v_ch11_romance_rose`
and sets `v_b2_ch1_interfere`. `b2ch7` is therefore a **convergence point** for four inherited
decisions. Verify each one produces a genuinely different emotional register, not just different
routing.

**Secondary — P6.** Check for stacked set pieces with no decompression between them.
