# Unit B3-U6 — Book 3, Chapter 6 (all three routes)

## Unit focus

Three routes, ~43.5k words — one of the largest units in the book, and structurally the most
lopsided in the project.

**The `b3ch6c` anomaly.** Your three files are wildly unequal: `b3ch6a` is 18.7k words / 47 scenes,
`b3ch6b` is 19.2k / 47, and **`b3ch6c` is 5.6k / 12** — roughly a third the size of its siblings.

`b3ch6c` sets nothing and reads four flags: `v_b3_ch6_kelrim`, `v_b3_ch6_plan`, `v_b3_ch6_labels`,
`v_b3_ch2_goblins`. It is a **pure convergence route** — an epilogue or short merge point.

**Do not pad it to match its siblings. Short is likely correct.** But do verify it is not merely a
truncated copy of a sibling's beats, and — because it reads four upstream flags and sets none — check
hard for Pattern 5: four decisions feeding one short file is the classic setup for a neutral merge
where nothing actually feels different.

**Primary target — P5.** This unit is the densest flag-authoring node in Book 3:

- `b3ch6a` sets `v_b3_ch6_plan`, **`v_b3_ch6_labels`** (read in `b3ch6c`, `b3ch8b`),
  `v_b3_ch4_billy`, `v_ch11_saved_rose`; reads `v_b3_ch6_kelrim`, `v_b3_ch5_fight`
- `b3ch6b` sets **`v_b3_ch6_kelrim`** (read in `b3ch6a`, `b3ch6c`, `b3ch9a`, `b3ch10a`),

- `b3ch6c` reads all of the above and sets nothing

Note that `6a` and `6b` **read each other's flags** — the routes are cross-coupled, not parallel.
Establish what each value means before you rewrite anything, and record all of it precisely in your
`CONTINUITY EXIT STATE`. Units B3-U8, B3-U9, and B3-U10 all depend on `v_b3_ch6_kelrim` and
`v_b3_ch6_plan`.
