# Unit B3-U9 — Book 3, Chapter 9 (all three routes)

## Unit focus

Three routes, ~46.6k words. **`b3ch9a` is one of the two convergence sinks in Book 3** — the files
that read state set almost everywhere else. The book-master has been told to review your report
hardest. Treat this unit as the highest-scrutiny assignment in the book.

**`b3ch9a` inbound flag load** — it reads: `v_b3_ch4_billy`, `v_b3_ch6_kelrim`, `v_b3_ch6_plan`,
`v_b3_ch8_king`, `v_b3_ch8_overseer2`, `v_b3_ch9_enemies`. Six upstream decisions land in one file.
It sets `v_b3_ch9_conclusions` (read in `9b`, `9c`) and `v_b3_ch8_overseer2`.

`b3ch9b` sets `v_b3_ch9_enemies` (read in `9a`, `b3ch10a`) and `v_b3_ch8_overseer2`; reads
`v_b3_ch9_conclusions`, `v_b3_ch8_overseer2`.
`b3ch9c` reads `v_b3_ch9_conclusions`, `v_b3_ch8_overseer2` and sets nothing.

Note that `9a` and `9b` **read each other's flags**. The routes are cross-coupled, not parallel.

**Primary target — P5, and this is the definitive test of it in Book 3.**

Six upstream decisions converging on one file is precisely where the original text will have written a
single neutral scene and gated only the routing. Your job is to make each combination *feel*
different. Go through every `#if` block in `b3ch9a` and ask: **if I were a player who chose the other
way, would this paragraph read differently to me?** If the answer is no, rewrite it.

You will not be able to give every permutation bespoke prose, and you should not try. Prioritise:
1. the flags with the widest reach — `v_b3_ch4_billy`, `v_b3_ch6_kelrim`
2. flags representing a moral or loyalty choice over a tactical one
3. scenes where a character's attitude toward Barry is at stake

**Secondary — P7.** `b3ch9a` averages **~524 prose words per scene**. Convergence files tend toward
recap and exposition — the exact conditions for white-room dialogue. Stage the long scenes: terrain,
weather, injury, physical business, bodies. Interleave, do not append. Calibration: Master Contract §7.

**Secondary — P3 residue.** `b3ch9c` runs **2.00 choices per scene** (70 / 35) — a sweep S1 priority
target. Verify every echo line matches its replaced label verbatim and every NPC reaction still lands
against the new line rather than a joke that no longer exists.

**Three-way variant sweeping** is mandatory. Report counts under `VARIANTS SWEPT`:


**Late-book cast canon.** Leila is **mute** — she writes glowing letters in midair. Watch for prose
that quietly gives her spoken dialogue or paraphrases her as *"Leila said that..."*. Embody her
through posture, writing speed, erased phrases, physical action. Flower and Illuna are **two people**;
never write one as the other's mood. Arraka's help always arrives wrapped in cruelty.
