# Unit B2-U7 — Book 2, Chapter 10 (both routes)

## Unit focus

Both routes of chapter 10, and **the two longest-scened files in Book 2**: `b2ch10a` averages ~479
prose words per scene and `b2ch10b` ~448. Both are well above the book average (~330).

**Primary target — P7, white-room dialogue.** Long scenes are where floating-head exposition lives:
extended debate with no terrain, no weather, no physical business, no bodies. With only 30 and 39
scenes respectively, you can afford to work every single one. For each, ask: could this be performed
on a bare stage? If yes, stage it — camp chores, weapon maintenance, injury, food, weather, posture,
the specific feel of the ground.

The two calibration passages to match are in the Master Contract §7 — the stream-water beat and the
wintergreen-salve beat. Both are conversations that became scenes because the characters were doing
something with their hands.

**Secondary — P6, pacing.** Long scenes also hide congestion: several beats stacked inside one scene
with no breath between them. This is late-book, approaching the finale, which is precisely where
Book 1 failed worst (Ch7 stacked eight set pieces). Check every death, reveal, and victory for a
runway before and a landing pad after.

**Secondary — P5.** Route asymmetry here is real:
- `b2ch10a` sets `v_b2_ch5_scimitar` and reads `v_b2_ch8_energizer`
- `b2ch10b` sets `v_ch11_saved_rose`, `v_second_book_purchased` and reads `v_b2_ch5_scimitar`

So `10a` feeds `10b`. Diff the scene lists and decide deliberately where the routes should read
differently rather than identically:

```sh
diff <(grep '^ID: ' data/en/b2ch10a.magium) <(grep '^ID: ' data/en/b2ch10b.magium)
```

## Do not

- Do not change any `choice(...)` label. Sweep S1 has already run.
- Never merge, reorder, or collapse an `#if` keyed on `v_second_book_purchased` — commercial gate.
- Do not touch `b2ch5*`, `b2ch8`, or `b2ch11*`.
- Do not pad. Staging a scene adds words; cutting over-explanation removes them. Net drift ±10%.
