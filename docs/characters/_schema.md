# Character Profile Schema

## Segment notes

Each segment file covers only its assigned story files and uses this structure for every roster character who appears:

```markdown
## Character: Name

### Facts and appearance
- Observation. [Scene ID]

### History
- Concise event or development. [Scene ID; route condition when relevant]

### Reactions
- Situation → characteristic response. [Scene ID]

### Tendencies
- Pattern supported by multiple moments in this segment. [Scene IDs]

### Relationships
- **Other character:** interaction pattern or change. [Scene ID]
```

- Separate direct observations from interpretation.
- Treat conditional routes as variants of one event, not multiple events.
- Record route-specific behavior only when it materially changes characterization.
- Attribute Flower and Illuna separately even though they share a body.
- Capture patterns and relationship changes, not scene-by-scene plot summaries.
- Use short paraphrases and scene IDs; avoid long quotations.
- Barry's narration and choices are evidence only when they reveal a recurring attitude or behavior.

## Final profiles

```markdown
# Name

## Overview
## Appearance
## Personality and tendencies
## Reactions and behavior
## Relationships
## History
```

- Merge segment notes in numerical order.
- Lead with stable patterns; mention development only when it changes those patterns.
- Keep history concise and broadly chronological.
- Minimize spoilers and route mechanics in the prose.
- Do not include inline evidence unless needed to clarify a route-dependent claim.
