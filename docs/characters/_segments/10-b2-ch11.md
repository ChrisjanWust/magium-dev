# Book 2, Chapter 11

## Character: Barry

### Facts and appearance
- Sent back into his thirteen-year-old body: shorter arms and hands, different clothes, no items or stat device. [B2-Ch11a-Cabin]
- Childhood home was an isolated wooden cabin with a river, farm patch, and barn. [B2-Ch11a-Cabin]
- Adopted after his birth parents were murdered; Helen and Jonathan raised him. [B2-Ch11a-Daylight]
- Sister Claire’s awakening turned her skin purple and deformed her body; he killed that banshee with a pitchfork. [B2-Ch11a-Cabin; B2-Ch11a-Wait]
- Half-lessathi; Arraka says any Magium affinity would be weaker and unusable without direct access. [B2-Ch11b-Malnourished]
- Almost no magic in his aura by Selkram’s measure. [B2-Ch11a-Attention]

### History
- The ritual forces him to relive Claire’s awakening until he recreates the true past: he was present, froze in fear, and only struck when his brother was about to die. [B2-Ch11a-Pitchfork]
- He had told himself for fifteen years that he arrived too late; recovering the memory explains his later disregard for danger. [B2-Ch11a-Eliminated]
- Optional loops of trying to save his parents all fail; the trial is not about changing the past. [B2-Ch11a-Trying; B2-Ch11a-Restrain]
- After the later kneel, flashbacks of that frozen moment harden into a vow to make Selkram pay. [B2-Ch11c-Forehead]
- Ends the book by asking Arraka how to kill a god. [B2-Ch11c-Ending]

### Reactions
- God demands kneeling → may kneel to protect the group, refuse, or insult the faceless form; refusal/insult gets his heart ripped out, then reversed. [B2-Ch11a-Kneel]
- Flower being tortured → can draw Selkram’s attention onto himself. [B2-Ch11a-Attention]
- Chance to rewrite the past → first impulse is to save his family and ignore the trial. [B2-Ch11a-Cabin]
- Arraka’s Magium near-erasure → existence-level dread worse than Arraka or Selkram. [B2-Ch11b-Warning]
- Leila withdrawn after her trial → checks on her, then gives space when she declines. [B2-Ch11c-Practice; B2-Ch11c-Explain]

### Tendencies
- Treats lethal risk as something to endure or walk into; Arraka calls his survival instincts defective. [B2-Ch11a-Attention; B2-Ch11a-Eliminated]
- Under prolonged pain, tries to “become one with” it rather than break. [B2-Ch11a-Kneel]
- Keeps asking what the Magium is; Arraka withholds the answer to frustrate him. [B2-Ch11a-Eliminated; B2-Ch11b-Warning]
- Player tone toward Rose’s new resolve can encourage, caution, or mock; Rose still proceeds. [B2-Ch11c-Encourage]

### Relationships
- **Daren:** After the break, pushes him past “prepare for the next ritual” toward killing Selkram; needles him for challenging Eiden but not a god. [B2-Ch11c-Ending]
- **Kate:** She screams his name when his heart is taken. [B2-Ch11a-Kneel]
- **Rose / revenant:** Can defend the revenant from Daren; on the saved route, witnessed her ritual and later talks her through the aftermath. [B2-Ch11c-Defend; B2-Ch11c-Herbalist]
- **Arraka:** Uses her as informant, endures her mockery, then seriously asks how gods die; she answers with excited laughter. [B2-Ch11a-Prophecy; B2-Ch11c-Ending]
- **Eiden:** Named in the disaster prophecy and in Daren’s remembered street scene as the one who once gave Barry attack power. [B2-Ch11a-Prophecy; B2-Ch11a-Luggage]
- **Illuna:** Contrasts Claire’s banshee with Illuna: Claire enjoyed the killings and cannot be reasoned with. [B2-Ch11a-Attack]
- **Leila:** Notices her habit of being overlooked; she is surprised he asks how she is. [B2-Ch11c-Practice]
- **Melindra:** Reads her kneeling as proof the threat is real. [B2-Ch11a-Prophecy2]
