# Book 1, chapters 8–10

Roster characters who appear: Barry, Daren, Kate, Rose, Hadrik, Flower, Illuna, Arraka, Eiden.

Leila and Melindra do not appear.

## Character: Barry

### Facts and appearance
- First-person narrator; lessathi, with partial paralysis resistance and above-average electricity resistance. [Ch10-Intro]
- Uses the stats device as a tool and, in the cell, as a light. [Ch8-Intro; Ch10-Intro]
- Fights with dagger and crossbow when magic is unavailable. [Ch8-Rush; Ch9-Nice; Ch10-Volunteer]

### History
- Wakes in the animal-kingdom ruins under a lessathi-style collar; later sent after the sacred chalice. [Ch8-Intro2; Ch8-Golden-rule]
- Can argue for talking to Eleya before breaking out; the group still escapes after her sentence. [Ch8-Talk; Ch8-Escape]
- Night conversation with Illuna (Rose present or arriving later) precedes Illuna’s stronger commitment to the ogre plan. [Ch9-Inventory; Ch10-Minimize]
- After the collars come off, Eiden traces the party and holds Barry to the lessathi-message bargain. [Ch10-Tracing; Ch10-Ending]

### Reactions
- Tactical caution (trap risk, hydra heart, shaman footprints) mixed with opportunistic jokes and glory-hogging. [Ch8-Talk; Ch8-Hearing-success; Ch10-Volunteer]
- Can mock Flower’s age or doubt her, then still intervene when Daren threatens the shared body. [Ch9-Acting; Ch9-Intervention]
- Variant: pockets treasury gems, or resists. [Ch10-Ambush]
- Eiden’s return produces panic rather than a plan. [Ch10-Ending]

### Tendencies
- Treats stats, observation, and bargaining as substitutes for magic. [Ch8-Languages-success; Ch8-Hearing-success; Ch10-Swear]
- Mediates fights he cannot win, then undercuts sincerity with sarcasm. [Ch9-Intervention; Ch10-Wrong]
- Choice lines often test cynicism vs. curiosity rather than change the event. [Ch9-Annoying; Ch9-Curious; Ch10-Number; Ch10-Betrayed]

### Relationships
- **Daren:** constant banter; Barry withholds or conditions the sword when Daren is not thinking straight; later hears Daren’s apology. [Ch8-Talk; Ch10-Sword; Ch10-Stubbornly]
- **Rose:** private camp talk; she calls his Tyrath stand gallant and blushes; he later keeps Daren away so Illuna can sleep. [Ch9-Whimpers; Ch9-Blushing; Ch10-Unconscious]
- **Kate:** she punches him for mocking Rose’s fear; he later checks on her about Diane. [Ch8-Seriously; Ch10-Lemon-cakes]
- **Illuna:** he seeks her out and later claims friendship; she rejects the label. [Ch9-Inventory; Ch9-Intervention]
- **Hadrik:** shares a toast and talks Magium motives. [Ch10-Drink; Ch10-Intervention]
- **Eiden:** still bound by the earlier life-for-a-message deal. [Ch10-Tracing]

## Character: Daren

### Facts and appearance
- Armored white mage; sword carries purification and other enchantments that still work under the collar. [Ch8-Banshee; Ch10-Curse]
- Shield is his usual defense; without it he is easier to throw. [Ch8-Smash; Ch10-Give2]

### History
- Tries to purify Flower’s “banshee problem” on first meeting; the ritual fails because Illuna is already awakened. [Ch8-Banshee; Ch8-Purification]
- Nearly duels Illuna at camp and again after the jailbreak; loses or is blocked, then is saved by her teleport. [Ch9-Intervention; Ch10-Give; Ch10-Teleport]
- Dispels the shaman’s death curse and calls the life-debt even. [Ch10-Curse]
- Admits he froze Kate out after the Beacon reveal; apologizes to her after the collars are gone. [Ch10-Stubbornly; Ch10-Sulking]

### Reactions
- Sarcastic “democracy” when Barry wants to wait; later gloats if that wait fails. [Ch8-Talk; Ch8-Escape]
- Immediate hostility to banshees; would rather risk killing Flower than leave Illuna unbound. [Ch8-Banshee; Ch9-Calm]
- Protective first: lifts Rose off the hydra; drops weapons when she is hostage. [Ch8-Surface; Ch9-Throw]
- Hangs from a breaking beam and lies that he has it under control so others will not climb down. [Ch10-Maintain]
- Eiden choking Rose: draws steel, then is paralyzed. [Ch10-Ending]

### Tendencies
- White-mage dogma outruns evidence; pride delays amends. [Ch8-Awakened; Ch10-Stubbornly]
- Blames Barry for bad guesses so he can follow them anyway. [Ch8-Dragonfly; Ch8-Beetle]
- Awkward, formal attempts at peace once he decides he was wrong. [Ch10-Spare; Ch10-Sulking]
- Recovers his usual needling as soon as the crisis eases. [Ch10-Generous]

### Relationships
- **Rose:** default protectee; guilt when leaving her behind goes wrong. [Ch8-Surface; Ch9-Throw]
- **Illuna:** specialty-enemy framing, then unpaid life-debt he refuses to interpret as friendship. [Ch9-Fight; Ch10-Quota; Ch10-Reason]
- **Kate:** days of silence she never noticed; late, strained apology. [Ch10-Stubbornly; Ch10-Sulking]
- **Barry:** relies on him as brake and sounding board. [Ch10-Sword; Ch10-Stubbornly]
- **Hadrik:** adventure-story rivalry; personality clash admitted over ale. [Ch9-Crossroads; Ch10-Magium]
- **Eiden:** open hostility on sight. [Ch10-Tracing]

## Character: Kate

### Facts and appearance
- Artificial mage; collar blocks new ice casting. Trained in martial arts without magic. [Ch8-Intro2; Ch8-Seriously]
- Knows Beacon collars and lessathi ethereal lore; names Diane as another ethereal. [Ch8-Intro2; Ch9-Space-out]
- In the ritual-world wolf fight, Barry reads her look as hunter, not just efficient fighter. [Ch10-Wolves]

### History
- Identifies the collars and the need for a control room. [Ch8-Intro2]
- Bonds with Flower over ethereals, Beacon experiments, and survival habits. [Ch9-Beacon; Ch9-Crossroads]
- Volunteers to guard Rose outside the fort (variant: Barry talks the group into taking Rose in). [Ch9-Strategy; Ch9-Take-Rose]
- Learns aura suppression from Illuna well enough to pass as human at a glance. [Ch10-Toast]
- After the chalice return, still intends to find Diane and asks to stay with the group. [Ch10-Ease; Ch10-Happened]

### Reactions
- Pragmatic: follow the local guide; smash walls only when Flower clears them. [Ch8-Disguise; Ch8-Dragonfly]
- Punches Barry for mocking Rose’s hydra freeze. [Ch8-Seriously]
- Softens toward Flower once she admits she could not let Eleya kill strangers. [Ch9-Intro]
- Throws Illuna her dagger when wolves pin her. [Ch10-Catch]
- Variant: a wolf claws her leg; Illuna heals it later. [Ch10-Dagger; Ch10-Spare]

### Tendencies
- Blunt analysis, then competence without ceremony (backpack inventory tap). [Ch9-Backpack]
- Protects Rose and treats “would you have me?” as a real question. [Ch9-Strategy; Ch10-Ease]
- Holds two truths about Diane: she tried to protect Kate, and her rashness still endangered them. [Ch10-Ease]

### Relationships
- **Rose:** friendship she feels responsible for after the collar-range rule. [Ch9-Crossroads]
- **Flower:** kinship as ethereal/artificial-mage; later invites her to stay. [Ch9-Beacon; Ch10-Generous]
- **Illuna:** student of aura work; asks about the massacre, then lets Arraka’s story stand. [Ch10-Colors; Ch10-Toast]
- **Daren:** did not register his silence; accepts the apology without treating it as a crisis. [Ch10-Sulking]
- **Barry:** intermittent irritation and later a quiet Diane talk. [Ch8-Seriously; Ch10-Lemon-cakes]
- **Diane:** absent, still the reason Kate remains in the tournament. [Ch9-Space-out; Ch10-Happened]

### Relationships

- **Elaria:** supporting; guilt over Molan’s father, then a promised visit. [Ch10-Elaria]

## Character: Flower

### Facts and appearance
- About four feet tall; long red hair to the waist; gray tank top; clown-like pants; no shoes; golden amulet. Looks about ten; turning thirty. [Ch8-Available; Ch8-Old]
- Green eyes when she holds the body; bright red when she readies to fight Eleya or Eiden. [Ch8-Golden-rule; Ch10-Ending]
- Fire is her element; water jets and healing belong to Illuna. Dual auras, one outworldly, one not; she suppresses them toward human-normal. [Ch8-Disguise; Ch8-Trap]
- Ethereal: lives from the soul’s side of the body; circus-trained acrobat. Body stopped aging at the fusion. [Ch9-Space-out; Ch8-Old]

### History
- Drops from a ceiling hole as ruins guide; slips into “we,” then denies Illuna. [Ch8-Training; Ch8-Disguise]
- Blocks Eleya and is assigned as the party’s guide as punishment. [Ch8-Control-room; Ch8-Ending]
- On the road she is a confident landmark guide; she and Illuna sleep in turns so Arraka is never unsupervised. [Ch9-Getting-along; Ch9-Dispel]
- Consents to Illuna’s two-day stasis for the “secret plan”; wakes after the chalice return. [Ch10-Minimize]
- Chooses to keep traveling with the group. [Ch10-Generous]
- On seeing Eiden, rages that he destroyed Olmnar; Illuna physically holds her back. [Ch10-Ending]

### Reactions
- Cheerful, socially clumsy, panic-laughs when the second person is noticed. [Ch8-Disguise; Ch9-Intro]
- Will not let Daren “save” her from Illuna; calls Illuna her friend. [Ch8-Banshee]
- Remembers traps late, then still offers healing and a soldier-march lead. [Ch8-Wait; Ch8-Alternative]
- Cannot abandon the party at the fort; keeps Arraka because unsupervised she can blast free. [Ch9-Worry; Ch9-Leader]
- Writes events down when the world moves while she sleeps. [Ch10-Reason]

### Tendencies
- Instant grasp of spectacle spells (food-conjure after one look at Eleya); hopeless at study-heavy healing. [Ch9-Sandwich]
- Childlike retorts and “I just like to blow stuff up,” with serious jailer duty underneath. [Ch9-Acting; Ch9-Leader]
- Trusts strangers quickly; treats group acceptance as unfamiliar. [Ch8-Disguise; Ch9-Intro]

### Relationships
- **Illuna (Petal):** fused souls; Flower named her before she would give a real name; happiest day was the awakening. [Ch8-Old; Ch9-Awakening; Ch9-Inventory]
- **Arraka:** jailer for twenty years; does not take Arraka’s oaths at face value, still values past saves. [Ch8-Arraka; Ch8-Lessathi]
- **Kate:** first real peer talk (ethereals, Beacon, aura tips). [Ch9-Beacon]
- **Daren:** likes Flower, not the banshee; Flower notices and understands Illuna’s silence. [Ch9-Introduce]
- **Eleya:** past help to the animals does not buy the party a pardon. [Ch8-Control-room]
- **Eiden:** twenty-year hatred tied to Olmnar, not to Illuna’s massacre name. [Ch10-Ending]

## Character: Illuna

### Facts and appearance
- Awakened banshee fused with Flower; blue eyes and a colder face when she has the body. [Ch8-Awakened; Ch9-Inventory]
- Water element: jets, whips, spears, food-conjure (learned from Flower), healing, golden cage, limited daily teleport. [Ch8-Trap; Ch10-Give; Ch10-Caverns]
- Real name hidden as “Petal” for years; ogres know her as Illuna of the sacred woods massacre. [Ch9-Inventory; Ch9-Traitorous]
- Complete control of visible emotion and aura most of the time; Arraka’s massacre story is the first violent fluctuation Barry notes. [Ch10-Sword; Ch10-Adorable]

### History
- Heals Hadrik, then stays silent with strangers; Flower calls it shyness. [Ch8-Awakened]
- Night watch: sandwich, no handshake, no bid for trust; later credited with upgrading the ogre plan to protect the party. [Ch9-Right; Ch10-Minimize]
- Puts Flower in stasis, cages the party, and “defects” so the shaman cannot read their shock. [Ch9-Traitorous; Ch10-Sword]
- Maps the fort with Arraka; cold, ceiling-drop kills on the way to the treasury. [Ch10-Apologizing; Ch10-Treasury]
- Saves Daren from the mine drop; takes the shaman’s death curse; Daren breaks it. [Ch10-Teleport; Ch10-Curse]
- Teaches aura suppression; sleeps only under Kate/Rose watch, still waking hourly for Arraka. [Ch10-Toast; Ch10-Stubbornly]
- Agrees, nervously, to travel on; stops Flower from attacking Eiden. [Ch10-Chain; Ch10-Ending]

### Reactions
- Cuts small talk; will not perform trustworthiness. [Ch9-Right; Ch9-Weather]
- Staged cruelty at the teleport is partly payback for a lost night of rest; she later apologizes to Rose. [Ch10-Apologizing]
- Combat is calculated (whips as decoy strength, wall-jumps using Flower’s athletics). [Ch10-Give2; Ch10-Treasury]
- Offers to hold the ogres while dying; laughs when she thinks Daren has come to purify her. [Ch10-Volunteer2; Ch10-Curse]
- Flustered by Rose’s hug; suspicious of Daren’s peacemaking. [Ch10-Curse; Ch10-Spare]

### Tendencies
- Temporary-alliance framing; denies friendship even after being defended. [Ch9-Guide; Ch10-Reason]
- Pragmatic fail-safes (kill-switch if both sleepers stay unconscious) credited as her idea. [Ch9-Dispel]
- Will use Flower’s body and reputation, then still spare Daren “for Flower’s sake.” [Ch9-Traitorous; Ch10-Give2]

### Relationships
- **Flower:** exasperated caretaker and strategist; Flower taught her the fox’s food spell. [Ch9-Sandwich; Ch10-Minimize]
- **Arraka:** former mentor who groomed then broke her; Illuna hates her and still needs the map. [Ch10-Adorable; Ch10-Hologram]
- **Daren:** mutual kill-readiness, then a silent even-debt; both snap “No!” when Flower says they are friends. [Ch9-Intervention; Ch10-Reason]
- **Rose:** refuses the handshake, then apologizes; Rose is the one she somewhat trusts. [Ch9-Inventory; Ch10-Unconscious]
- **Barry:** useful, not a friend. [Ch9-Intervention]
- **Kate:** willing teacher once asked. [Ch10-Toast]
- **Eiden:** recognizes her after twenty years; Illuna’s flash of fear overrides Flower’s assault. [Ch10-Ending]

## Character: Arraka

### Facts and appearance
- Amulet-bound banshee; voice is three women laughing or speaking at once. [Ch8-Disguise]
- Can swap a sinister aura for a “joy and kindness” one and a little-girl voice. [Ch8-Arraka]
- Trapped by lessathi twenty years ago; Flower and Illuna jail her because unsupervised she can slowly channel a small explosion and escape. [Ch8-Arraka; Ch9-Acting]
- Flower says she heard that Arraka led stillwaters in the Still Winter while fused with one; Eleya and the Creator then barred Arraka from humanoid hosts. Flower’s fused aura no longer counts as human, so Arraka could possess that body. [Ch9-Leader]
- Magical sense can paint living creatures on a hologram map, including masked/invisible ogres; closing the amulet is her excuse to drop the map. [Ch10-Apologizing; Ch10-Adorable]
- Motion-sick if the amulet is spun; that is an amulet-sense problem, not an old host-body one. [Ch10-Chain]

### History
- First act on-page is outing Flower as a banshee, then lying about the elevator lever (“swear on my mother”). [Ch8-Disguise; Ch8-Arraka]
- Channels an explosion while chatting with Hadrik; Flower dispels it. [Ch9-Dispel]
- Warns that the ogres are invisible (help the party, or enjoy the chaos). [Ch9-Open]
- Hours in the library with Illuna to build the fort map; later adds the mine tunnels and flags a hidden blast trap. [Ch10-Apologizing; Ch10-Wait]
- Tells the sacred-woods story to wound Illuna in front of the group. [Ch10-Adorable]
- Feints a “neck technique” so Hadrik can punch the captain’s chest. [Ch10-Quota]
- Locket left open as a reward; she claims full membership and a heads-on-spikes camp plan. [Ch10-Reason]

### Reactions
- Hysterical laughter; offers to be freed in exchange for silence or practice-ogres. [Ch10-Number; Ch9-Acting]
- Help is real when it serves a joke, a kill, or keeping Flower alive as her only remaining humanoid host. [Ch9-Life-with-Arraka; Ch9-Open]
- Mentors as a long con: motherly years, then betrayal timed for maximum pain; she “grew fond” of Illuna enough to reshape her instead of killing her. [Ch10-Adorable]

### Tendencies
- Lies, dark jokes, and Still Winter anecdotes as social mode. [Ch8-Arraka; Ch10-Wrong]
- Plays the fool; Flower and Illuna both treat her as cunning when she bothers. [Ch9-Smartest]
- Kill-per-hour complaints when ignored. [Ch10-Chain]

### Relationships
- **Flower:** not taken seriously; still kept close as jailer and last viable host. [Ch8-Lessathi; Ch9-Leader]
- **Illuna:** star pupil she destroyed; Illuna will close the amulet even if the map dies. [Ch10-Adorable]
- **Hadrik:** only audience she likes; she says she will probably kill them all if freed, after the beer. [Ch10-Quota]
- **Kate:** Kate says Arraka’s aura sends chills down her spine; Arraka enjoys the ethereal label. [Ch9-Acting; Ch9-Beacon]
- **Eleya / the Creator:** the pair who bound her from humanoid possession. [Ch9-Leader]

## Character: Eiden

### Facts and appearance
- Stillwater: boyish, eyes closed, hands in pockets, polite smile; appears from empty air. [Ch10-Tracing]
- Magical sense covers Varathia; auras give identity, distance, lies, age, race, mental state, and can be used to halt aging. [Ch10-Tracing]
- Can choke Rose by closing her airways and paralyze the rest of the party at once. [Ch10-Ending]

### History
- Finds the camp after the collars are gone, asking whether Barry delivered the lessathi message. [Ch10-Tracing]
- Treats a missed delivery as Barry not taking him seriously; chooses to kill a friend as a lesson. [Ch10-Never; Ch10-Electrocuted]
- Greets “Illuna of the sacred woods massacre,” then asks Illuna to stop Flower; Flower names him as the destroyer of Olmnar. [Ch10-Ending]

### Reactions
- Soft “I only came to help” over a frown; cheerful while escalating. [Ch10-Tracing; Ch10-Ending]
- Dodges Flower’s fire without casting; patience thins, then he delegates the stop to Illuna. [Ch10-Ending]

### Tendencies
- Bargains and lessons enforced by overwhelming, casual force. [Ch10-Never]
- Knows the fused pair’s history and uses the massacre title, not Flower’s name, until the fight. [Ch10-Ending]

### Relationships
- **Barry:** still collecting on the dragon-fight bargain. [Ch10-Tracing]
- **Daren:** hostility answered with paralysis. [Ch10-Ending]
- **Rose:** chosen pressure point. [Ch10-Ending]
- **Illuna / Flower:** twenty-year acquaintance; Flower’s Olmnar charge is separate from Arraka’s massacre story. [Ch10-Ending]
