## Character: Barry

### Facts and appearance
- Studies a personal notebook of creature lore and keeps a stat device; Leila has one too. [B2-Ch04a-Technically; B2-Ch04a-Private]
- Fights with a dagger Daren enchanted; may take one or both of Velgos’s enchanted scimitars after the troll dies. [B2-Ch04b-Marauder; B2-Ch05a-Troll-dead]
- Has piloted gnomish flying machines before and can man an acid-coated turret. [B2-Ch05b-Contraption; B2-Ch05b-Trapped]

### History
- After the caverns, he researches troll regeneration and links it to Eiden’s remarks about auras. [B2-Ch04a-Knowledge]
- He withholds the lessathi king-deal from friends (either to avoid anger or to wait until they are safe). [B2-Ch04a-Knowledge]
- He arranges a Kate–Leila meeting before the first horn. [B2-Ch04a-Trace]
- In the first arena wave he either charges alone, waits with the group, or shares cavern intel; Leila and (sometimes) Bruce finish the field with him. [B2-Ch04b-Charge; B2-Ch04b-Further]
- He douses Velgos in dwarven ale and has Leila throw a torch, then helps force repeated beheadings until the troll’s regeneration fails. [B2-Ch05a-Flammable; B2-Ch05a-Troll-final]
- Forced into a death-duel with Daren, he follows Daren’s surrender and can mock the ambassador or stage a fake kill. [B2-Ch05b-Disarmed; B2-Ch05b-Peasant; B2-Ch05b-Finish]
- Optional extra duel: he can refuse, kill, or spare the sixth-division axe-fighter; sparing leads to a public denunciation of the crowd. [B2-Ch05b-Refuse; B2-Ch05b-Spare]
- During Tyrath’s attack he pilots or guns Golmyck’s aircraft and helps drop the wing-binding rope. [B2-Ch05b-Pilot; B2-Ch05b-Gunner]

### Reactions
- Teasing Illuna’s eyes vs. defending her “means well” vs. baiting Arraka — all collapse to the same later briefing of friends. [B2-Ch04a-Fat; B2-Ch04a-Means; B2-Ch04a-Worried]
- Sharing cavern intel vs. hoarding it: Leila looks disappointed if he withholds after offering to share. [B2-Ch04a-Caverns; B2-Ch04a-Overheard]
- Before the Daren duel he loops on “don’t panic”; Daren’s calm surrender is the plan he failed to invent. [B2-Ch05b-Disarmed]
- If he uses the division mage as a decoy, he shrugs off the betrayal afterward. [B2-Ch05a-Mage; B2-Ch05a-Troll-Aftermath]
- If he spares the axe-fighter, he snaps at the booing crowd and lectures them as “mindless sheep.” [B2-Ch05b-Spare]

### Tendencies
- Treats notebooks, overheard guards, and observed fights as the real weapons; he will leave the stands to fetch pages. [B2-Ch04a-Backpack; B2-Ch04a-Read]
- Prefers planned openings over instinct and will bait creatures with their own behaviors. [B2-Ch04b-Lure; B2-Ch04b-Cobras]
- Distrusts the division as teammates while still using them as tools. [B2-Ch04a-Concentrate; B2-Ch05a-Mage]

- Dry mockery under official pressure (ambassador, announcer). [B2-Ch05b-Peasant; B2-Ch05b-Doctor]

### Relationships

- **Kate:** Tests her memory of Leila, then engineers the reunion and promises to keep Leila safe. [B2-Ch04a-Fat; B2-Ch04a-Safe]
- **Daren:** Accepts “stay alive, don’t win” as the brief; later leans on Daren’s enchanted blade and rule-reading. [B2-Ch04a-Reunion; B2-Ch05b-Peasant]

- **Eiden:** Uses an offhand aura lecture as research; later flies against Tyrath while Eiden spectates and then intervenes. [B2-Ch04a-Knowledge; B2-Ch05b-Serious]
