# Book 1, chapters 4–6

Roster who appear: Barry, Daren, Kate, Rose, Eiden. Flower, Illuna, Hadrik, Arraka, Leila, and Melindra do not appear.

---

## Character: Barry

### Facts and appearance
- Ordinary human using a stat booster that works unusually well on him; Eiden later implies the device matches his specifically weak aura. [Ch4-Daren-spare; Ch6-Ending]
- Freezes and shakes when a real stillwater appears, then tries to sound confident. [Ch4-Beacon]
- Magical aura weaker than a normal human’s. [Ch6-Ending]

### History
- Trades with Eiden for the party’s lives: a suicide bargain (game over) or delivering “Remember the still winter” to dark-blue-robed hawk-sun brooch wearers. [Ch4-Accept; Ch4-Propose]
- May use Dave’s attack on Eiden as cleanup, intervene, or fight Dave himself depending on prior face-shown and Kate’s condition. [Ch4-Dave-vs-Eiden; Ch4-Gruesome-fate; Ch4-Strategy]
- If he lied about why he joined, Kate pushes him to confess; admitting cools Daren, staying silent keeps the lie until Rose’s presence collapses it. [Ch5-Private-lie; Ch5-Admit-lie; Ch6-Secure]
- Hears a local scream: tournament-first inaction vs. rushing to rescue. Either path still leads to meeting Rose. [Ch5-Ignore; Ch5-Rescue]
- With Eiden’s charged device, fights Tyrath for a few minutes as a temporary mage; win/loss both end with Eiden trapping the dragon. [Ch6-Power-up; Ch6-Win-fight; Ch6-Eiden-vs-dragon]

### Reactions
- Overwhelming stillwater threat → panic, then bargain or self-sacrifice rather than freeze permanently. [Ch4-Beacon; Ch4-Propose]
- Dave about to be slaughtered by Eiden → either pragmatic non-intervention or stepping out to prevent a gruesome death. [Ch4-Fewer-enemies; Ch4-Gruesome-fate]
- Caught lying to Daren → sweat, stalled speech, then a blunt Magium-dream confession. [Ch6-Secure]
- Dragon fight looking unwinnable → keeps searching for an angle, then accepts Eiden’s power-up and plans or improvises. [Ch6-Villain; Ch6-Think]
- Kate’s leftover burn scar (if she was already badly hurt) → optional tactless insult or a tattoo comparison that actually comforts her. [Ch6-Mask; Ch6-Tattoo]

### Tendencies
- Default voice is sarcastic and tactless even under annihilation; Eiden names this as what he “likes.” [Ch4-Truth; Ch5-Reckless; Ch6-Profess]
- Survival calculus first (rules, trades, stats), then loyalty when a companion is actually dying or abandoned. [Ch5-Scream; Ch4-Propose; Ch6-Think]
- Will bluff, stall, or flirt rather than sit in an earnest conversation. [Ch4-Face-shown; Ch6-Flirt]
- Curious about secrets (Beacon, Eiden’s age, lessathi) and will poke them even when it costs trust. [Ch5-Private-truth; Ch5-Older]

### Relationships
- **Daren:** Values him as the better man; will die or deal for him, but withholds the Magium motive until forced. Daren’s silence after the lie bothers him. [Ch4-Accept; Ch5-Admit-lie; Ch6-Travel]
- **Kate:** Treats her as a person, not a banshee; she thanks him for that. He still needles her and withholds his own secrets. Her closing look after the aura remark leaves him expecting more group conflict. [Ch6-Maybe; Ch6-Ending]
- **Rose:** Struck by her looks on first sight; later talks her through deer-guilt (optional) and fields her matchmaking questions with deflection or passes. [Ch5-Goblins; Ch6-Met; Ch6-Flirt]
- **Eiden:** Fear mixed with defiant bargaining; becomes the stillwater’s designated messenger and later a powered-up test subject. [Ch4-Propose; Ch4-Truth; Ch6-Eiden-vs-dragon]

## Character: Daren

### Facts and appearance
- Armored healer already rumored as “the healer in armor”; item enchanting is his specialty (eyes go yellow in trance). [Ch4-Intro2; Ch6-Packing]
- Heavy sleep wards that Eiden still bypasses with a gesture. [Ch5-Intro]
- Carries anti-magical rope and will lend Barry a spare dagger while scolding him for not scavenging. [Ch4-Daren-spare; Ch4-Strategy]

### History
- Rants that the party ignored strategy, then cools and debates tournament ethics with Barry. [Ch4-Intro]
- Challenges Eiden’s stillwater pose, then gets crushed into the rock and later put to sleep mid-charge—repeatedly. [Ch4-Beacon; Ch4-Propose; Ch4-Kate-meets-Eiden]
- Prefers binding Dave and leaving fate to the gods; if persuaded, allows Kate to execute. Spare routes, Dave teleports away. [Ch4-Agree; Ch4-Persuade; Ch4-Busy2]
- Immediately escorts Rose home to deliver the oildrip cure, overriding consultation. [Ch5-Disappear]
- After Barry’s lie is exposed (or admitted), goes cold: short answers or silence on the road, still fights and heals with the group. [Ch5-Admit-lie; Ch6-Wasnt; Ch6-Travel]
- In the dragon fight, shields the party, gets snatched, stabs the claw, then tries to cover Kate. [Ch6-Morning; Ch6-Villain]
- Heals everyone afterward; if Kate’s burn is too deep, admits the limit. [Ch6-Healing]

### Reactions
- Organizers risking lives → moral outrage, refuses to believe a last-man-standing design. [Ch4-Intro; Ch4-Intro2]
- Apparent stillwater / idealist bait → answers with “good worth fighting for,” then attacks anyway after the trade. [Ch4-Beacon; Ch4-Propose]
- Defeated enemy → spare and bind, not execute, unless argued into the cost of escape. [Ch4-Busy2; Ch4-Daren-kill]
- Local in danger → runs toward the scream; goblins flee at the sight of him. [Ch5-Ignore; Ch5-Rescue]
- Barry’s private talk with Kate → wink and thumbs-up; misreads the mood. [Ch5-Threat]
- Eiden reappearing → shout and charge, even after multiple sleep-drops. [Ch4-Kate-meets-Eiden; Ch6-Eiden-leaves]

### Tendencies
- Idealist who will still fight a force he cannot beat rather than let it walk. [Ch4-Propose]
- Talks most in group travel; jokes to defuse (Barry “smitten,” stat device as a kid’s pet toy) unless the lie has shut him down. [Ch6-Backstory; Ch5-Reckless; Ch6-Stats]
- Strategy meetings and escort logistics over waiting in the wild. [Ch4-Intro; Ch5-Consult; Ch6-Intro]
- Gods-and-honor framing for mercy; bristles at mockery of the gods. [Ch4-Agree]

### Relationships
- **Barry:** Covers for “shy” Barry with Eiden, heals him, enchants his pack, and feels betrayed when the Magium motive surfaces. Still hands over his sword if Barry asks during Tyrath. [Ch4-Intro2; Ch5-Intro; Ch6-Sword]
- **Kate:** Heals her first when she’s cut; she doubts he truly sees her as human. He still fights beside her and jokes about Barry’s crush. [Ch4-Intro; Ch6-Maybe; Ch6-Healing]
- **Rose:** Volunteers the escort, enchants her pack, insists on the fastest (riskier) road for the sick. [Ch5-Disappear; Ch6-Secure]
- **Eiden:** Named “idealist” and crushed for it; treats him as an enemy to stop, not a savior. [Ch4-Beacon; Ch6-Eiden-leaves]

## Character: Kate

### Facts and appearance
- Ice mage; Beacon of Hope survivor (Eiden’s label; she will not explain). [Ch4-Intro2; Ch5-Private-truth]
- Facial features less soft than Rose’s; Barry calls her beauty “feral” only on the route where Tyrath’s burn still scars her face. [Ch5-Goblins; Ch6-Healing]
- If still unrecovered from the crossbow ambush, she sleeps through Dave, staggers on the road, and can take a lasting left-face burn from Tyrath. [Ch4-Barry-vs-Dave; Ch6-Secure; Ch6-Villain]
- Knows the hawk-sun robes as a king-backed underground group and looks at the ground when saying so. [Ch5-Wake-Kate]

### History
- After Dave’s insults, stacks ice swords and kills the crew; will finish Dave if Barry backs her, otherwise yields to Daren. [Ch4-Barry-vs-Dave; Ch4-Persuade]
- Questions why Eiden spared Daren; redirects “what are you after?” when honesty fails. [Ch4-Kate-meets-Eiden]
- Calls the still-winter line a likely threat; argues they lack a viable anti-Eiden plan and should use Barry’s bought time. [Ch5-Threat]
- Confronts Barry if he lied to Daren; if he told the truth, she still won’t unpack the Beacon. [Ch5-Private-lie; Ch5-Private-truth]
- Opposes escorting a local until Rose physically blocks a mage attacking Kate; then she honors the escort. [Ch5-Great; Ch5-Monster]
- Cools the travel air with ice; later panics Rose during dragonfire and keeps casting after ice fails. [Ch6-Intro; Ch6-Morning]
- After the fight, names Eiden an enemy whose mercy is whim; then links Barry’s device to a sub-human aura and goes silent. [Ch6-Simple; Ch6-Ending]

### Reactions
- Sleep-deprived / stench of Dave’s men → vicious mockery before violence. [Ch4-Barry-vs-Dave]
- Being called banshee/whore/monster → rage in combat; misery and looking down when strangers scream it unprovoked. [Ch4-Barry-vs-Dave; Ch5-Great]
- Personal questions (Beacon, robes) → “it’s complicated,” delay, walk away. [Ch4-Kate-meets-Eiden; Ch5-Private-truth]
- Barry’s tactlessness after she tries to be kind → smack or punch. [Ch5-Reckless; Ch5-Heroism]
- Romance rumor → tense private ask, then a clear not-interested (or relief if denied). Ice to the throat if Barry jokes she confessed. [Ch6-Intro; Ch6-Maybe; Ch6-Profess]
- Incomplete heal / new scar → dry joke that people already called her a monster. [Ch6-Healing]

### Tendencies
- Practical risk assessment over heroics (non-interference rule, don’t pick a fight with Eiden yet). [Ch5-Ignore; Ch5-Threat]
- Hides history that could isolate her; promises later clarity rather than inventing a cover. [Ch5-Private-truth]
- Softens around Rose (faint smiles, escort reversal, travel talk) while remaining evasive about her own past. [Ch6-Travel; Ch6-Backstory]
- Will tank pain and keep fighting; resolve hardens after the burn. [Ch6-Villain]

### Relationships
- **Barry:** Trusts his lack of monster-fear; still scolds his lies and his mouth. Ends the segment newly afraid of what his aura implies. [Ch6-Maybe; Ch5-Private-lie; Ch6-Ending]
- **Daren:** Uses him as healer/tank, doubts he believes she’s human, expects an eventual fight. [Ch6-Maybe; Ch5-Threat]
- **Rose:** First person besides Barry who doesn’t look at her as other; Kate’s gratitude after the body-block is unusually open. [Ch5-Monster; Ch6-Maybe]
- **Eiden:** Suspicious from the first clap; treats later rescue as the same capriciousness as the morning assault. [Ch4-Kate-meets-Eiden; Ch6-Simple]

## Character: Rose

### Facts and appearance
- Mid-twenties local herbalist: long auburn hair, thin waist, warm smile, soft features, light-brown eyes, auburn dress. [Ch5-Goblins]
- Speaks the deer tongue and enough of an ancient/goblin tongue to teach berry rules. [Ch6-Packing; Ch5-Goblins2]
- Not a mage; Eiden labels her a time weaver after she stays unfrozen. She feels no different and assumes the spell glitched. [Ch6-Time-weaver; Ch6-Healing]
- Volunteers in her town; supports siblings (9 and 8) after her father’s death; mother died in childbirth; amicable ex-husband still sends some money. [Ch5-Creator; Ch6-Backstory]

### History
- Alone collecting rare herbs for an oildrip outbreak the poor cannot pay to treat; goblins surround her. [Ch5-Humble; Ch5-Creator]
- After being saved, still feeds and instructs the goblins who attacked her. [Ch5-Goblins2]
- Explains Creator / golden fox history, food-craft collapse, hunting taboo breaking down. [Ch5-Creator]
- Puts herself between Kate and Clive; the anti-local-harm spell yanks him out. [Ch5-Monster]
- Optional deer reunion: cannot meet Elaria’s eyes because someone from her town killed Molan’s father in the human–animal war. [Ch6-Packing; Ch6-Met]
- Travels the fast monster-heavy road at Daren’s insistence; freezes in terror when Tyrath arrives, then greets Eiden politely inside stopped time. [Ch6-Secure; Ch6-Morning; Ch6-Time-weaver]
- Defends Eiden as a savior until Kate’s “on a whim” framing deflates her. [Ch6-Eiden-leaves; Ch6-Simple]

### Reactions
- Rescue / thanks → elegant bow, polite smile, then practical herbalist talk. [Ch5-Work]
- Starving goblins → help and “don’t attack defenseless people,” not punishment. [Ch5-Goblins2]
- Friends arguing over escorting her → asks Daren not to fight them on her account; claims she knows the safe roads. [Ch5-Great]
- Kate under a sword → steps in citing a life-debt and her Varathian status. [Ch5-Monster]
- Kate/Barry bickering → giggle; compares them to her little siblings. [Ch5-Reckless]
- Guilt at the deer cave → smile drops; later she can take blunt comfort or accept that cutting them off was selfish. [Ch6-Packing; Ch6-Presence]
- Sudden flirtation → chuckle, delay, or a clean decline; one line reminds her of her ex. [Ch6-Flirt; Ch6-Passion]
- Dragon → shivering, hugging her legs; after the barrier burst she is knocked out. [Ch6-Morning; Ch6-Villain]

### Tendencies
- Default warmth and peacemaking; will still stand in a killing blow’s path. [Ch5-Monster; Ch6-Travel]
- Explains local politics and ecology patiently; downplays her stories as “boring.” [Ch5-Disappear; Ch6-Monster]
- Curious about magecraft (watches Daren enchant, blushes when caught). [Ch6-Packing]
- Believes the best of helpers (Eiden as friend) until given a concrete counter-example. [Ch6-Eiden-leaves]

### Relationships
- **Barry:** Grateful; optional deer talk and fruit-break questions about Kate. Treats passes as premature, not cruel. [Ch6-Met; Ch6-Flirt]
- **Kate:** Defends her as a person, asks if she needs rest when she’s weak, draws out Kate’s rare smiles. [Ch5-Monster; Ch6-Secure]
- **Daren:** Accepts escort and pack enchantment; tries to broker talk when he ices Barry out. [Ch6-Secure; Ch6-Travel]
- **Eiden:** Introduces herself, asks if he’s Barry’s friend, calls after him when Daren shouts. [Ch6-Time-weaver; Ch6-Eiden-leaves]
- **Elaria / Molan:** Old herb-ointment friends; she withdrew after the wartime killing and still apologizes on sight. [Ch6-Packing; Ch6-Met]

## Character: Eiden

### Facts and appearance
- Looks late teens, loose sleep-like tunic and pants, hands in pockets, eyes shut; aura as weak as the crossbowmen’s. [Ch4-Intro2]
- Can see; closed his eyes long ago at “the ugliness of humanity,” using magical sense instead (also beats illusions). [Ch4-Beacon]
- Stillwater: air crush, sleep through heavy wards, teleport, fire that ignores trees, time stop, presence masking. [Ch4-Beacon; Ch4-Fewer-enemies; Ch6-Time-weaver]
- Seeking people in dark blue robes with a hawk holding the sun; message: “Remember the still winter.” [Ch4-Propose]
- Knows Beacon of Hope, stat boosters, lessathi/ancient traps, and Tyrath of old. [Ch4-Intro2; Ch4-Daren-spare; Ch6-Eiden-vs-dragon2]
- Wrote a star-message in a dragon-hunter tongue to bait hunters at Tyrath for sport. [Ch6-Power-up]

### History
- Greets Barry as a fellow stillwater; impressed if Barry already dropped the bluff. [Ch4-Intro2; Ch4-Beacon]
- Tests Daren’s idealism by grinding him into stone, then offers Barry a life-for-life trade. [Ch4-Beacon]
- Accepts the messenger trade; if Daren still attacks, sleeps him and leaves. [Ch4-Propose]
- Massacres Dave’s crew if they surround him, or spectates the party’s fight and claps. Punishes Barry for using him as bait (kneecaps) but praises the sarcasm. [Ch4-Fewer-enemies; Ch4-Truth]
- Kills a failing “mailman” Dave, or Barry if Barry lies about it. [Ch4-Busy2; Ch4-Lie]
- Watches the dragon fight masked, freezes time when Tyrath’s gloat is too cartoonish, charges Barry’s device for a five-minute stillwater-level taste, then either fights Tyrath or lets Barry warm an old lessathi dragon-prison trap. [Ch6-Villain; Ch6-Power-up; Ch6-Eiden-vs-dragon2]
- Fakes hurt when Daren shouts, thanks Rose, and fades—possibly still nearby. [Ch6-Eiden-leaves]

### Reactions
- Daren’s sarcasm or “blind stillwater” jab → polite smile, then a philosophical trap and overwhelming force. [Ch4-Intro2; Ch4-Beacon]
- Thugs trying to intimidate him → wider smile; he enjoys the massacre. [Ch4-Dave-vs-Eiden]
- Barry lying to his face → instant kill. [Ch4-Lie]
- Barry admitting he used him → lesson in pain, then the smile returns. [Ch4-Truth]
- Tyrath’s villain speech → cannot let that win; frames it as taste. [Ch6-Villain]
- Rose unfrozen → interest, congratulations, “rooting for” the odd party. [Ch6-Time-weaver]
- Barry wasting the dump of energy on maximized stats → “disappointing,” then a lecture on not wasting magic. [Ch6-Lose-fight]
- Old dragon calling him friend/enemy → hug joke, fire returned, trap sprung. [Ch6-Eiden-vs-dragon]

### Tendencies
- Treats killing, mercy, and aid as trades, jokes, or whims, while insisting he honors the deal he made. [Ch4-Propose; Ch4-Kate-meets-Eiden; Ch6-Simple]
- Withholds answers because “where would the fun be?” [Ch4-Daren-spare; Ch6-Power-up]
- Speaks as if centuries old (humanity’s ugliness, millennia-old traps, maybe a century since Tyrath) inside a boy’s body. [Ch4-Beacon; Ch6-Eiden-vs-dragon]
- Keeps eyes closed in populated places; smile is the tell before violence. [Ch4-Intro2; Ch4-Fewer-enemies]

### Relationships
- **Barry:** Messenger, amusement, and “great potential” / “big plans”; saves him to keep the game going, not out of kindness. [Ch4-Truth; Ch6-Eiden-vs-dragon]
- **Daren:** “Healer in armor,” idealist toy; will not kill him after the trade, will sleep him whenever he charges. [Ch4-Beacon; Ch4-Propose]
- **Kate:** Names her Beacon survivor unprompted; dodges her motive question. [Ch4-Kate-meets-Eiden]
- **Rose:** First to call her a time weaver; accepts her courtesy at face value when leaving. [Ch6-Time-weaver; Ch6-Eiden-leaves]
- **Tyrath:** Old acquaintance/enemy; baited him with the star message and treats the rematch as reunion theater. [Ch6-Power-up; Ch6-Eiden-vs-dragon]
