# Book 1, Chapter 11

Sources: `data/en/ch11a.magium`, `data/en/ch11b.magium`

Rose-gated material (Ella, the muffin visit, the optional night with Barry) is a variant of the same Thilias stay, not a second timeline. The sacrificial-trap ending is one event with a saved or revenant outcome.

## Character: Barry

### Facts and appearance
- Carries a crossbow, dagger, and the stat device; can lock the device so others only see the screen. [Ch11a-Thousands; Ch11a-Toy]
- Tournament protection normally blocks even imagining harm to locals; Eiden’s area spell removes that block for anyone nearby. [Ch11a-Power; Ch11a-Wonderful]
- Emergency mode on the device temporarily unlocks hidden Magical power / Magical knowledge by siphoning leaked stillwater energy; weaker and less versatile than Eiden’s earlier boost. [Ch11b-Emergency]

### History
- Presses Eiden on Olmnar and age, then asks Arraka to flag invisible Eiden. [Ch11a-Intro; Ch11a-Intro2]
- At the slave beating, either takes Eiden’s power to intervene or stays silent until Daren does. If he intervenes, he can kill the noble or try to drive him off; Eiden incinerates the noble when he threatens Rose’s house. [Ch11a-Favor; Ch11a-Power; Ch11a-Silent; Ch11a-Thousands]
- If Ella is saved, he can joke, free her, or send her to Rose’s house; Rose then has to translate “freedom” into something survivable. [Ch11a-Boots; Ch11a-Free; Ch11a-House]
- Optional muffin-night: he can confirm lingering attraction or walk it back. Confirmation leads to one night Rose frames as thanks, not a marriage. [Ch11a-Feelings; Ch11a-Racing; Ch11a-Romance]
- During the marketplace fight he can freeze-all (fatal without enough premonition) or hunt originals; later solos or teams against the skeletons. [Ch11b-Freeze; Ch11b-Concentrate; Ch11b-Alone; Ch11b-Team]
- After Arraka’s massacre, attacking her is the only non-death choice; Rose’s rewind then lets him act again. Max hearing, perception, and agility lets him stop the post-death stab. [Ch11b-Dagger; Ch11b-Weaver; Ch11b-Hole]

### Reactions
- Friend in visible distress → mock, offer space, or leave her alone; all three still move the group into the city. [Ch11a-Definitely; Ch11a-Break; Ch11a-Alone]
- Eiden’s moral trap → either grab the offered power or treat the beating as not his business. [Ch11a-Power; Ch11a-Nothing; Ch11a-Silent]
- Eiden asking how he will save thousands of slaves → vow to save them all, throw the job back at Eiden, or refuse all-or-nothing logic. [Ch11a-Difference]
- Suzie blaming herself for Ella’s collapse → soothe her or fetch Daren instead. [Ch11a-Comfort; Ch11a-Someone-else]
- Kate about to ice every clone → stop her if a premonition fires; otherwise the rebound kills him. [Ch11b-Freeze]
- Arraka demanding a fight after killing the others → draw the dagger rather than refuse (refusal is a death). [Ch11b-Dagger; Ch11b-Satisfaction]
- City fight with borrowed magic → weigh civilian risk against raw power, or stall versus rush the necromancer. [Ch11b-Strongest; Ch11b-Civilians; Ch11b-Buy; Ch11b-Directly]

### Tendencies
- Treats Eiden as a source of answers and tools, then undercuts the lecture with a joke or a counter-question. [Ch11a-Intro; Ch11a-Difference]
- Defaults to action when a victim is in front of him, then undercuts the gravity with a joke (boots, spinning Arraka). [Ch11a-Boots; Ch11b-Spin]
- Combat thinking is trap-first: do not smash the obvious target; look for the real caster, the rebound, the loophole. [Ch11b-Concentrate; Ch11b-Square; Ch11b-Emergency]
- Uses the stat device as both weapon and curiosity (Suzie’s toy, emergency mode). [Ch11a-Toy; Ch11b-Emergency]

### Relationships
- **Eiden:** Curious, then used; Eiden expects Barry to take the power and later opens his eyes at Barry’s answers. [Ch11a-Power; Ch11a-Difference]
- **Rose:** Relies on her as local guide; if he saved Ella she thanks him privately and may sleep with him as repayment. He can accept or defuse it without cruelty. [Ch11a-Knock; Ch11a-Feelings2; Ch11a-Racing2]
- **Daren:** Ignores Daren’s morning scolding; later credits Daren for killing the Rose gossip. [Ch11a-Romance; Ch11a-Serious]
- **Kate:** She yanks his hair for the boot joke; he still takes her Beacon reading of Ella seriously. [Ch11a-Boots; Ch11a-Loyalty]
- **Arraka:** Bargains for Eiden-watch, then reluctantly uses her barrier advice after she has already killed him once in the undone timeline. [Ch11a-Intro2; Ch11b-Strongest2]
- **Hadrik:** If the slave dies, Hadrik briefly treats Barry as having become Arraka; Barry does not re-litigate it. [Ch11a-Business; Ch11a-Clown]

## Character: Daren

### Facts and appearance
- Tall, armored healer; locals and posters name him “the healer in armor.” [Ch11a-Boots; Ch11a-Poster]
- First meeting with Eiden: charged him knowing he would lose and was put to sleep. [Ch11a-Definitely]
- Healing can clear bruises and fever but not fatigue, and not a completed heart-stab. [Ch11a-Fever; Ch11b-Hole]

### History
- After the teleport, first concern is whether Rose needs healing. [Ch11a-Intro2]
- Slavery in the street is the break: he moves to intervene, accepts Rose’s tournament warning, then refuses Eiden’s “free” power and tries talk, then money. [Ch11a-Slave; Ch11a-Nothing; Ch11a-Silent]
- Variant — girl dies: he weeps over the body, asks Eiden for the power, beheads the noble, and walks past Rose. [Ch11a-Wonderful]
- Variant — Barry saves her: Daren is already withdrawn; he still heals Ella when called. [Ch11a-Details; Ch11a-Comfort]
- Isolates in his room until morning, then becomes the one hurrying Barry downstairs. [Ch11a-Toy; Ch11a-Serious]
- In the ambush he checks Rose’s pulse, charges the first illusions, and later demands Arraka return Flower and Illuna; Arraka dusts him in seconds. [Ch11a-Hostages; Ch11b-Clones; Ch11b-Sonic]
- After the rewind he sends Rose off the line, holds the party barrier, and either heals everyone (Rose lives) or takes the death as his failed escort promise (Rose dies). [Ch11b-Safety; Ch11b-Hole; Ch11b-Revenant]

### Reactions
- Visible cruelty he cannot legally stop → fist-shaking rage, then persuasion instead of Eiden’s shortcut. [Ch11a-Slave; Ch11a-Nothing]
- Persuasion failing into murder → collapse, then immediate lethal force once the block is gone. [Ch11a-Silent; Ch11a-Wonderful]
- Rose trying to speak after he killed the noble → walk past her. [Ch11a-Wonderful]
- Ella fainted → first question is why nobody made her rest; still heals first. [Ch11a-Comfort]
- Arraka claiming Flower and Illuna are absorbed → sword out, no interest in her vendetta. [Ch11b-Sonic]
- Rose stabbed → frantic stacked heals, then self-blame. [Ch11b-Hole]
- Revenant wearing Rose’s face → frozen, then a late charge after her flicker of the old Rose. [Ch11b-Revenant]
- Hadrik calling Rose’s death honorable → furious rejection of “primitive values.” [Ch11b-Hole]

### Tendencies
- Will charge a stronger enemy on principle (Eiden then, Arraka now) even when he knows the odds. [Ch11a-Definitely; Ch11b-Sonic]
- Needs someone to save before he can function; when that fails he withdraws or turns dark. [Ch11a-Wonderful; Ch11a-Toy]
- Treats healing as duty that overrides his isolation. [Ch11a-Comfort; Ch11b-Ending]
- Measures himself by promises to protect, not by Hadrik’s honor talk. [Ch11b-Hole]

### Relationships
- **Rose:** Escorts her home, snaps at her over slavery, then either shuts her out after the killing or keeps working in her house. Her death is his failed vow; her rewind is “saved the entire world.” [Ch11a-Slave; Ch11a-Wonderful; Ch11b-Hole]
- **Eiden:** Open hatred; will not ask him for power until the girl is already dead. [Ch11a-Favor; Ch11a-Silent]
- **Hadrik:** Denies any feud; acid when teased about armor; explodes when Hadrik eulogizes Rose. [Ch11a-Families; Ch11b-Hole]
- **Flower / Illuna:** Relates to Flower’s hopeless charge; later tries to force Arraka to give both back. [Ch11a-Definitely; Ch11b-Sonic]
- **Barry:** Wakes him, doubts the sudden magic is Eiden again, then follows the plan. [Ch11a-Serious; Ch11b-Strongest]
- **Kate:** Present for the slave scene; no comfort offered either way. [Ch11a-Silent]

- Lives in her ex-husband Enrique’s two-floor mansion with yard; she calls her life modest because she did not buy it. Siblings Suzie (8) and Kevin (9); she used to ask Enrique to check on them. [Ch11a-Modest; Ch11a-Passion]

If he recants she smiles, takes muffins for Suzie, and goes. [Ch11a-Knock; Ch11a-Feelings; Ch11a-Racing2]
- Morning-after, Flower outs her leaving Barry’s room; she panics, then grabs the hospital route when Daren coughs over it. [Ch11a-Romance]
