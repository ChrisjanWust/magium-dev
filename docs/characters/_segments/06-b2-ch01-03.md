- About twenty; a head shorter than Barry; frail-looking; long silver hair (once black; Beacon experiments bleached it). Beige blouse, short leggings, wooden sandals, two belt knives, fresh bruises. [B2-Ch02a-Princess]
- Lessathi outcast; device also flags nearby stat boosters and lets her read energy flow and “spiky” lying auras. [B2-Ch02a-Outcasts; B2-Ch02a-Better]
- Cannot swim. [B2-Ch02a-Swim]

### History
- Beacon failed experiment; birth father volunteered her. A caretaker (now “father”) broke her out; they hid, then lived under a suggestion-field cabin. [B2-Ch02a-Natural; B2-Ch02a-Hiding]
- After Meridith banned outcast hunts they moved to a city; father was framed into slavery. Tournament stats let her max speed, strength, and combat technique (cap 3) and free him; she was caught covering his escape. [B2-Ch02a-Outcasts; B2-Ch02a-Meridith]
- Device gift at sixteen: first only a few words, later almost all her thoughts. [B2-Ch02a-Meridith]
- In Barry’s division: stares, then writes; private cell talk; finds lessathi wall-switches; falls into the lake trap with him. [B2-Ch02a-Princess2; B2-Ch02a-Passage]
- Fights cavern beasts (kill route) with acrobatics and thrown daggers. [B2-Ch02a-Acrobatic]
- Stays for the legal arena path instead of Fyron’s teleport; owner escorts both back as “stars of the show.” [B2-Ch03a-Unique; B2-Ch03a-Stars]
