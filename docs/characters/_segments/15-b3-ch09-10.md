# Book 3, Chapters 9–10

Ch9b (Overseer alliance) and Ch9c (king/Peter alliance) are mutually exclusive after the shared ethereal displacement in Ch9a. Record route-specific behavior only where it changes characterization. Shared aftermath: Flower’s abyss rescue, lingering plane-shift, then Ch10 travel toward Meridith.

## Character: Barry

### Facts and appearance
- Soul is ejected into the ether as a bodiless consciousness; he can will hands, then a full body, into existence. [B3-Ch09a-Calm; B3-Ch09a-Feather]
- Casts real spells on the ethereal plane from memorized particle flows, not from unlocked stats. [B3-Ch09a-Stance; B3-Ch09a-Lunatic]
- His ethereal mind is an empty cube in both routes. In Ch9b only, he sits with legs tucked and Flower says she once lived in a cube too. [B3-Ch09b-Cube; B3-Ch09c-Pieces]
- After the abyss, he keeps briefly flipping into the cube and later trains dual-plane awareness without blinking. [B3-Ch09b-Fluke; B3-Ch10a-Flow]
- Meridith’s wand matches their auras: he is her sister’s half-lessathi son, age twenty-eight. [B3-Ch10c-Secret; B3-Ch10c-Prince]

### History
- Optional talk with Jack before battle; Daren reads it as second thoughts if they already chose the Overseer. [B3-Ch09a-Jack]
- Learns one element in Flower’s mind (Flower / Illuna / Melindra / Kate) before the ether fight. [B3-Ch09a-Fire; B3-Ch09a-Water; B3-Ch09a-Air; B3-Ch09a-Ice]
- **Ch9b:** Fights Peter and the king with Flower (and optionally the Overseer). Can spare them for an anti-magic cell or execute the unconscious pair. [B3-Ch09b-Kill; B3-Ch09b-Cube]
- **Ch9c:** Teams with Peter and the king against the Overseer’s hydra; dual-controls his earthen body; Hadrik destroys the core. [B3-Ch09c-Upwards; B3-Ch09c-Indebted]
- Shared: ether attack; Flower teleports him into the ethereal abyss; opening his eyes there kills him. [B3-Ch09b-Visualize; B3-Ch09c-Phrase]
- Arraka begins teaching him her Magium-plane spell branch because he can now cast on the ethereal plane. [B3-Ch10b-Absorb]
- Learns he is Meridith’s nephew; biological parents were killed on Heksol’s orders after she helped them flee. [B3-Ch10c-Prince]

### Reactions
- Ether without a body → overthinks breathing and nearly dies; only reasoning that he has already survived without air saves him. [B3-Ch09a-Breathe; B3-Ch09a-Tree]
- First chance to cast → treats it as earned study, not a stat-device gift. [B3-Ch09a-Ice; B3-Ch09a-Lunatic]
- **Ch9b, unconscious enemies →** optional cold execution vs capture; Flower cannot talk him out of the kill once he commits. [B3-Ch09b-Kill]

### Tendencies
- Turns theory, feints, and remembered manuals into combat tools (dispels, anti-sound, pillar paralysis). [B3-Ch09b-Invent; B3-Ch09c-Maintenance]
- Hides plane-shift episodes until they become obvious, then practices them as a skill. [B3-Ch09b-Fluke; B3-Ch10a-Abyss; B3-Ch10a-Flow]

### Relationships
