# Book 1, Chapters 1–3

Roster who appear: Barry, Daren, Kate. Flower, Illuna, Arraka, Eiden, Hadrik, Rose, Leila, and Melindra do not appear.

## Character: Barry

### Facts and appearance
- Twenty-eight; self-described ordinary human with no talents, skills, or academic background. [Ch1-Intro1]
- Lifelong obsession, since age five, with becoming a mage; joined the Varathia tournament for the Magium, which he believes can turn a human into a mage. [Ch1-Intro1; Ch1-Ordinary]
- Carries a fortune-teller “stat booster” that spends ambient magic to raise physical and other stats. [Ch2-Stats]
- Fifteen years earlier he met a banshee up close; high-pitched laughter still triggers panic. [Ch2-Intro]
- Not a stillwater; Cutthroat Dave assumes he is because he has a human-like aura in a mage tournament. [Ch1-Cutthroat Dave]

### History
- Opening feeling is a player variant (excited / calm / afraid); all three still commit to winning. [Ch1-Intro2]
- Bluffs Dave with an Olmnar-destroyer implication, then impulsively stops Dave from finishing Daren. [Ch1-Imply; Ch1-Imply2]
- Identity to Daren is a variant: keep the stillwater cover, or admit he is human (truth / forced / sick sister). If he keeps the cover, Daren forces the confession after the first group fight. [Ch1-Humor; Ch1-Ordinary; Ch2-Battle-end; Ch2-Confession]
- Stops Daren from “purifying” Kate, citing firsthand banshee experience. [Ch2-Intro]
- First real battle is the bearded enchanter and his sons; combat role is a variant (hide behind Daren, kite, fight, or call Kate). [Ch2-Battle-begins]
- Optional ancient-languages route: bargains with talking deer for cave shelter. [Ch2-Deer; Ch2-Protection]
- On watch, realizes he has long admired Daren as a fairy-tale hero; that is why he defended him. [Ch3-Vantage]
- Kate wakes him at night for private help (debt collection or a debt she will owe). He lies that Daren, not he, judged her not a banshee. [Ch3-Kate-debt; Ch3-Bugging]
- Confirms leftover magic at a battlefield is electric, not a standard element; Kate smiles and uses his name. [Ch3-Daren-worried]
- Ambush by the duke of Bagelberry’s non-mage battalion: either he spots the sleep dart and fights with Kate, fetches Daren after Kate is darted (only if he learned her suggestion-spell workaround), or solos the army. [Ch3-Dart; Ch3-Call-Daren; Ch3-Give]
- First kill (if taken) is a variant attitude: easy, necessary, or horrifying; later lethality toward the duke and troops is also a variant. [Ch3-First-kill; Ch3-Decide-Killed; Ch3-Ice-hand]

### Reactions
- Mortal bluff succeeding → heart racing and self-rebuke for “playing hero,” without undoing the act. [Ch1-Imply2]
- Banshee-like aura → trauma flash, then a reasoned distinction that Kate’s feel is different. [Ch2-Intro]
- Fresh corpses → variant: too busy surviving, pity without letting it in, or near-vomiting. [Ch3-Busy; Ch3-Sorry; Ch3-Puke]
- Kate’s unexpected warmth after the electric-trace reading → surprise that she can be “nice.” [Ch3-Daren-worried]
- Kate executing a surrendered duke (when he tried to spare him) → moral protest, then acceptance of her revenge/risk argument. [Ch3-Saved-Spare-Take]
- Same executions after he already chose no survivors → agreement rather than shock. [Ch3-Alone-Leader-Return]
- Kate’s despair warning about becoming a mage → insistence that she must understand his drive; he does not quit. [Ch3-Born]

### Tendencies
- Opportunistic stillwater bluff and knowledge of mage lore used as weapons. [Ch1-Cutthroat Dave; Ch1-Imply]
- Protects others first, lectures himself afterward. [Ch1-Imply2; Ch2-Intro]
- Social deflection: jokes, sarcasm, or half-truths when cornered (Kate whisper variants; deer “fooling around”). [Ch2-Pass; Ch2-Travels; Ch3-Bugging]
- Will lie to preserve an alliance (credits Daren for sparing Kate). [Ch3-Bugging]
- Treats the tournament as a problem of tactics and stats, not glory. [Ch2-Stats; Ch3-Tactics]
- Killing ethic is a wide player spectrum, from nonlethal at personal cost to leaving no survivors; “hide and hope they leave” is fatal. [Ch3-Hide; Ch3-Incapacitate; Ch3-Every]

### Relationships
- **Daren:** Life-debt protector he admires; Barry risks himself for him, then leans on him in combat. Honesty about being human is optional; Magium-sharing pledge is optional if Barry told the Magium-dream truth. [Ch1-Imply2; Ch3-Vantage; Ch3-Worried]
- **Kate:** Starts as the person he refuses to let Daren kill; she treats him as a stillwater threat. By chapter 3 she needs his obscure-magic knowledge and, after he saves or relieves her, switches to first-name debt talk. He hides that he, not Daren, is why she was not purified. [Ch2-Intro; Ch2-Battle-end2; Ch3-Bugging; Ch3-Saved-Return]
- **Cutthroat Dave:** Enemy created by the stillwater bluff and Daren’s rescue; Dave vows to remember both of them. [Ch1-Imply2]
- **Elaria / Molan (supporting, deer route):** Treated as people to bargain with or (variant) threaten; Daren and Kate cannot follow the speech. [Ch2-Protection; Ch2-Delicious]

## Character: Daren

### Facts and appearance
- Bald, dark-skinned, mid-forties, X-shaped forehead scar; heavy armor, sword and shield. [Ch1-Intro2]
- Known as the legendary savior of the eastern continent, the healer in armor; tales include fighting ten rock golems for a child. [Ch1-Intro2]
- White-magic healer; can close wounds with a visible white light. Healing needs him calm. [Ch1-Humor; Ch2-Battle-begins]
- Never mastered the physical half of the shield spell; armor and enchanted weapons compensate. Strong vs elementalists, summoners, shapeshifters; weak vs weapon enchanters. [Ch1-Weakness; Ch3-Tactics]
- Carries a short-range magical transceiver. [Ch3-Seduce]
- Poor at spotting illusion tells; wants Barry’s observation for that. [Ch3-Intro2]
- Off-armor in camp: more comfortable clothes. [Ch3-Intro]

### History
- Loses to Cutthroat Dave’s living knives at armor joints; Barry’s bluff saves him. Immediately vows tournament-long protection, then breaks the solemn tone laughing at the name “Barry.” [Ch1-Imply2]
- Volunteers his greatest weakness to a stranger because that stranger saved him. [Ch1-Weakness]
- Recruits Kate on reputation request; refuses any escort arrangement that drops Barry. [Ch2-Escort]
- Horrified that participants are already killing with “no rules”; rushes to shield Barry in the family ambush. [Ch2-Fallen-trees]
- If Barry still claimed stillwater, Daren is angry after the fight that the lie nearly got them killed. If Barry already confessed, he instead asks Kate why she needs an escort. [Ch2-Battle-end]
- Skeptical of the stat device and of talking animals; yields when Kate confirms the device. Pushes for a shared combat plan before sleep. [Ch3-Intro; Ch3-Tactics]
- Night watch: travel stories (giants, Iknar plague, green wyvern); Magium framed as a last chance to actually save the world. Hands Barry the transceiver and, reluctantly, promises not to purify Kate until she transforms. [Ch3-Vantage; Ch3-Seduce]
- If fetched during the dart ambush: heals Kate incompletely, carries her back, scolds the split. [Ch3-Call-Daren]

### Reactions
- Life-debt → instant loyalty, oversharing, and “Adventure awaits!” energy. [Ch1-Imply2; Ch2-Against]
- Party tension → de-escalates (“Whoa, whoa”) then imposes a three-person deal. [Ch2-Against]
- Corpses → lasting chest pain; says he never gets used to death. [Ch3-Vantage]
- Kate’s aura → default “purify the banshee”; Barry’s testimony plus a look and a sigh is enough to delay. [Ch2-Intro]
- Barry’s Magium-as-human-dream (truth route) → disbelief, then eye-to-eye acceptance and a warning that bluffing will not win. [Ch1-Truth]
- Forced/sister cover stories → practical rescue plans and extra protection promises. [Ch1-Forced; Ch1-Sister]
- Split from the group → safety lecture aimed at Barry, not himself. [Ch3-Barrier]
- Kate’s “born human, became a mage” claim (if Barry repeats it) → disbelief; still treats banshee awakening as inevitable. [Ch3-Mage]

### Tendencies
- Heroic register that collapses into embarrassment, laughter, or sarcasm. [Ch1-Imply2; Ch3-Seduce]
- Trusts quickly after a moral act; reads people as good or not. [Ch1-Weakness]
- Open about his own weaknesses; shrugs when Kate will not share hers. [Ch3-Tactics]
- Protector-first combat: intercepts blows meant for Barry even when it costs him. [Ch2-Battle-human; Ch2-Hide]
- Conventional dogma: women cannot be born mages; Kate is a delayed banshee. [Ch2-Intro; Ch3-Seduce]
- Matchmaking teasing about Barry and Kate that he drops when the banshee warning starts. [Ch3-Worried]

### Relationships
- **Barry:** Debtor, shield, and would-be partner in Magium use (truth route) or in getting Barry off the continent / finding a cure (cover routes). Admires Barry’s first-battle handling. [Ch1-Imply2; Ch3-Busy; Ch3-Worried]
- **Kate:** Grants escort on legend-seeking; watches her as a potential banshee while promising Barry he will wait. Does not grasp why someone that strong asked for protection. [Ch2-Escort; Ch2-Battle-end; Ch3-Seduce]
- **Cutthroat Dave:** Humiliating loss; Dave’s threat includes him. [Ch1-Imply2]

## Character: Kate

### Facts and appearance
- Slim, tall, early twenties; long black hair, fair skin, serious face; leather pants, long-sleeved white blouse, green hooded cloak. [Ch2-Intro]
- Strong magical aura; ice elementalist (shields, spheres, cones, daggers, dome, fans, ice hand, suggestion-area barrier). [Ch2-Battle-human; Ch3-Daren-worried; Ch3-Dart]
- Born on Varathia, left, used the tournament to return; looking for a friend, not the Magium. [Ch2-Escort; Ch3-Awkward]
- Not born a mage; became one later, and says that was never her intention. [Ch3-Born]
- Knows how stat boosters work; partial ancient language; suggestion magic used against banshee hunters (large area, twice a day, hours). [Ch3-Intro; Ch3-Fox; Ch3-Daren-worried]
- Has magical weaknesses she will not name. [Ch3-Tactics]

### History
- Approaches Daren for an escort; objects to travelling with a “stillwater”; complies only because Daren will not split the party. Gives her name after being asked. [Ch2-Escort; Ch2-Against]
- Whisper-answer variants: ignores looks/pass jokes, distrusts a vague lie, or nearly leaves in tears if told they were debating whether she is a banshee—until Daren says they already decided she is not. [Ch2-Looks; Ch2-Banshee]
- In the family fight, ice-blocks a sword volley and snaps at being called both undefended woman and banshee; then overwhelms the sword mage and snipes the fleeing son. [Ch2-Battle-begins; Ch2-Avoid2]
- After the fight, privately grabs Barry’s shirt: either “I see through the human act” or “you owe me” if he called her for help. [Ch2-Battle-end2]
- Confirms Barry’s device to Daren without claiming to trust Barry. Refuses to list her weaknesses. [Ch3-Intro; Ch3-Tactics]
- Wakes Barry in secret: needs his reading of obscure magic and a private question. Reveals Varathian origin if asked about the device. [Ch3-Kate-debt; Ch3-Awkward]
- Wants to know why Daren did not purify her; believes Barry because “your face gives you away.” Shuts down the Magium-as-dream conversation after warning of agony. [Ch3-Bugging; Ch3-Born]
- Battlefield traces of electric elemental magic produce her first slight smile and a corrected “stillw-- Barry.” [Ch3-Daren-worried]
- Sleep-dart ambush: ice dome; if undarted, uses Barry as spotter or gives him an ice dagger; if darted, holds until Daren or until she massacres the battalion alone. [Ch3-Dart; Ch3-Call-Daren; Ch3-Plan]
- Executes the captured duke if Barry tries to spare him; executes surrendered troops if Barry captured the leader. Frames it as preventing later hunts, not revenge. [Ch3-Saved-Spare-Take; Ch3-Alone-Leader-Return]
- After Barry saves her from the dart or clears the army, acknowledges a debt (or a second debt). [Ch3-Saved-Return; Ch3-Alone-Crossbowmen-Return]

### Reactions
- Banshee label → visible hurt (tears, downcast twitch) then combat fury. [Ch2-Banshee; Ch2-Fallen-trees]
- Barry’s stillwater status → Olmnar mass-murder accusation; will not drop suspicion after one battle. [Ch2-Against]
- Daren’s reputation → immediate trust of him, not of Barry. [Ch2-Quick]
- Outnumbered non-mages → contempt (“do you think you can win by outnumbering?”) plus lethal follow-through. [Ch3-Dart]
- Sleep dart / exhaustion → lies “I’m alright,” then grim endurance and “I’ve been in worse.” [Ch3-Dart]
- Barry’s sarcastic worry about Daren → withholds the barrier explanation; a polite “why not?” gets the full lecture. [Ch3-Daren-worried]
- Surprise competence from Barry → honest “I didn’t expect you to pull it off.” [Ch3-Alone-Leader-Return]

### Tendencies
- Guarded, curt, watches her back; information is rationed. [Ch2-Against; Ch3-Tactics]
- Keeps a running debt ledger and collects or pays it promptly. [Ch2-Always; Ch3-Kate-debt; Ch3-Saved-Return]
- Combat: overwhelming ice, no prisoners when she judges them a future hunt. [Ch2-Ask-Kate; Ch3-Saved-Spare-Take]
- Survival pragmatism over Daren-style mercy; still sought Daren because she hoped his kindness would not mean a death sentence. [Ch3-Bugging; Ch3-Alone-Leader-Return]
- Avoids putting purification ideas in the white mage’s head; questions Barry instead. [Ch3-Bugging]
- Will use Barry’s knowledge while still calling him stillwater until a specific help lands. [Ch2-Battle-end2; Ch3-Daren-worried]

### Relationships
- **Daren:** Chosen escort and white-mage threat in one; trusts his legend, fears his purification, will not share weaknesses with him. [Ch2-Escort; Ch3-Bugging; Ch3-Tactics]
- **Barry:** Distrusted stillwater → useful specialist → named ally who is owed. She never hears that he is the reason Daren held back. [Ch2-Battle-end2; Ch3-Bugging; Ch3-Saved-Return]
- **Missing friend (unnamed):** stated reason for being on the continent; electric residual magic is treated as a lead. [Ch2-Escort; Ch3-Daren-worried]
- **Banshee hunters / Olmnar stillwaters:** prior hunted life; stillwaters are mass murderers in her moral map. [Ch2-Against; Ch3-Daren-worried]
