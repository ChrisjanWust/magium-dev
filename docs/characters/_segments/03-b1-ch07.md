# Book 1, Chapter 7

Roster present: Barry, Daren, Kate, Rose, Hadrik, Eiden.

## Character: Barry

### Facts and appearance
- Weak magical aura; Kate treats that as lessathi evidence. [Ch7-Diane]
- Carries Daren’s emergency transceiver (three taps). [Ch7-Intro]
- Fifteen years ago, after his sister awakened as a banshee and killed their parents, he impaled her with a pitchfork to stop her from killing his younger brother. [Ch7-Sister]
- Unusually calm with a blade at his throat; attributes it to the last day’s violence. [Ch7-Love; Ch7-Helped]
- Diane later identifies him as lessathi and knocks him out last. [Ch7-Ending]

### History
- Talks Kate down (no-tap) or, after signaling Daren, stops the purification by invoking Daren’s promise. [Ch7-Sister; Ch7-Promised]
- First person Kate tells the Beacon story to. [Ch7-Sorry-beacon; Ch7-Sorry-lessathi]
- Choice variants: stillwater bluff vs denial; help Kate vs wait; accept Hadrik’s fistfight vs refuse. [Ch7-Convince; Ch7-Help; Ch7-Accept; Ch7-Refuse]
- Deduces Hadrik is a giant shapeshifted small and proves it with a giant-clan salute. [Ch7-Accept; Ch7-Home-address]
- Diane charges him to keep Kate safe. [Ch7-Ending]

### Reactions
- Kate’s interrogation → jokes, talk-it-through, or a hidden distress signal; lethal threat does not make him refuse the private walk. [Ch7-Borrow; Ch7-Sister; Ch7-Tap]
- Daren about to purify Kate → shouts the promise, then bargains for minutes alone with her. [Ch7-Promised]
- Crowd treating him as a stillwater → leans into the rumor or tries honesty; either way he stays in the fight. [Ch7-Convince; Ch7-Not-convince; Ch7-Intimidate]
- Kate isolated by a mob → will not abandon her; “don’t help” is still wait-and-see, not walk away. [Ch7-Help; Ch7-No-help]
- After danger passes → downplays the knife, the duel, and the debt. [Ch7-Knife; Ch7-Welcome]

### Tendencies
- Defuses lethal distrust with talk, jokes, and a handshake instead of matching Kate’s violence. [Ch7-Sister; Ch7-Idiot; Ch7-Numb]
- Puts keeping Kate in the group above his own safety and Daren’s risk calculus. [Ch7-Promised; Ch7-Help; Ch7-Ask-me-anything]
- Reckless self-insertion: stillwater act, rushing a healer, accepting a giant’s spar. [Ch7-Intimidate; Ch7-Surprise; Ch7-Accept]
- Uses book-knowledge as a social weapon (lessathi banner, giant credo) once he has a read. [Ch7-Lessathi; Ch7-Home-address]

### Relationships
- **Kate:** From knife-at-throat to “are we still friends?”; he frames travel with her as safer than the day’s other threats. She calls him an idiot and later thanks him for the Diane lead. [Ch7-Idiot; Ch7-Numb; Ch7-Welcome]
- **Daren:** Uses the secret transceiver and the purification promise; elbows Daren into following Kate. Daren treats him as the one who needs protecting from her. [Ch7-Intro; Ch7-Promised; Ch7-Ask-me-anything]
- **Rose:** Brief, practical contact via transceiver; she is the one who later locks the group onto Kate’s errand. [Ch7-Okay; Ch7-Ask-me-anything]
- **Eiden:** Wary of being used as spectacle; once mimics Eiden’s casual walk to intimidate a healer. [Ch7-Watch-call2; Ch7-Intimidate]
- **Hadrik:** Sought-after opponent, then traveling companion; refuses to report Hadrik’s giant secret because Barry is equally out of place. [Ch7-In-or-out; Ch7-Home-address]
- **Diane:** Does not know him; still assigns him Kate’s safety after reading him as lessathi. [Ch7-Ending]

## Character: Daren

### Facts and appearance
- Gave Barry a transceiver Kate was never offered; three taps mean danger. [Ch7-Intro]
- Elementalist specialist: shield holds against Kate’s ice, sword absorbs and returns elements, a technique severs her magic for thirty minutes. [Ch7-Daren-vs-Kate]
- Heals Kate’s ankle and later restores the magic he cut. [Ch7-Tap-truth]
- Known enough as “the great hero Daren” that Azarius delays intervening to watch him. [Ch7-Dwarf-vs-fire]

### History
- If Barry taps: stalks in, fights Kate, wounds her, starts the purification, then yields when Barry cites the promise. [Ch7-Kill; Ch7-Promised]
- Throws Rose the transceiver the instant they are teleported. [Ch7-Agree-Kate]
- After the Beacon reveal, goes quiet for an unusually long stretch. [Ch7-Ask-me-anything]

### Reactions
- Kate’s two-hour coldness → dead-serious “three taps,” not a public confrontation. [Ch7-Intro]
- Kate nearly killing Barry → purify now; “too dangerous to be left alone”; apology to Barry, not to her. [Ch7-Kill]
- Out-argued by Barry → grumbles, then complies (talk time; stay to watch Eiden). [Ch7-Promised; Ch7-Watch-not-call]
- Kate’s snark after the stab → ignores it, heals her anyway, postpones the interrogation. [Ch7-Tap-truth]
- Kate exposed as a “banshee” → “Not this again”; argues they should not risk themselves for someone who will not be purified; cites Barry’s new neck bruise if he saw it. [Ch7-Go-call]
- Azarius naming Kate a woman mage → blurted disbelief, then fluster when called out for traveling with someone he still treats as a banshee. [Ch7-Woman-mage]
- Rose screaming at dragon-Hadrik → immediate, practical reassurance. [Ch7-Refuse]
- Kate’s goodbye → “We are?” until Barry elbows him into “of course.” [Ch7-Ask-me-anything]

### Tendencies
- Protects Barry first; Kate is a threat to be managed, not a person he looks at as human. [Ch7-Intro; Ch7-Love; Ch7-Go-call]
- Combat-first when trust breaks; still honors a stated promise and still heals her. [Ch7-Promised; Ch7-Tap-truth]
- Risk-averse in crowds: leave while others watch, do not die for Kate’s refusal to be purified. [Ch7-Wait2; Ch7-Go-call]
- Shock sticks; the Beacon news silences his usual talking. [Ch7-Ask-me-anything]

### Relationships
- **Barry:** Partner and liability; secret comms, front-line defense, frustration when Barry keeps shielding Kate. [Ch7-Intro; Ch7-Vulnerable; Ch7-Go-call]
- **Kate:** Mutual contempt under a travel truce; he has waited to purify her, and she knows it. Healing her does not equal trust. [Ch7-Kill; Ch7-Tap-truth]
- **Rose:** Entrusts her with the transceiver; soothes her dragon panic. [Ch7-Agree-Kate; Ch7-Refuse]
- **Eiden:** Prior humiliation remembered; wants either escape or martial intel, not another fight. [Ch7-Watch-not-call; Ch7-Watch-call2]

## Character: Kate

### Facts and appearance
- Ice dagger, platforms, beams, walls, club, cage, and—when going all out—an ice dragon that looks almost alive. [Ch7-Intro; Ch7-Daren-vs-Kate; Ch7-Kate-vs-Hadrik]
- Green cloak and hood used to pass as a man in a mage crowd. [Ch7-Watch-not-call; Ch7-Go-call]
- Cold stare as default; tears and a trembling hand when the kill will not go through. [Ch7-Intro; Ch7-Kill]
- Varathia-born, abandoned, Beacon orphan; magic from lessathi experiments; dumped in a jungle at twelve as a “reject.” [Ch7-Beacon]
- Returned to Varathia only for Diane. [Ch7-Sorry-beacon]

### History
- Two hours of silence after the stat-device talk, then a private ambush: she believes Barry is lessathi and has Diane. [Ch7-Intro; Ch7-Yes]
- First time she has told anyone the Beacon story. [Ch7-Sorry-beacon]
- After the handshake, she still insists on explaining before they rejoin the others. [Ch7-Idiot; Ch7-Numb]
- Publicly claims she came alone so Barry and Daren can run. [Ch7-Go-call]
- Bargains with Hadrik (Barry’s fists, or her magic) to reach Diane; offers pay he refuses. [Ch7-In-or-out; Ch7-Refuse; Ch7-Kate-vs-Hadrik]
- Diane rejects rescue; Kate refuses to leave and is shocked down. [Ch7-Spirits]

### Reactions
- Barry joking or saying he loves her → rage (tree-punch, “is everything a joke?”); a serious face about his sister is what lands. [Ch7-Love; Ch7-Sister]
- Pity → “Do I look like I need your pity?”, then a sigh and walk-back: she did not know what to expect. [Ch7-Sorry-beacon]
- Daren’s “surrender” → she will not; once beaten she goes silent like someone awaiting execution. [Ch7-Kill; Ch7-Daren-vs-Kate]
- Failed kill / being saved → apology, then clings to Barry’s arm on the walk; asks why he saved her. [Ch7-Promised; Ch7-Knife]
- Friendship offered after the knife → shock, “you’re insane / an idiot,” then a firm handshake. [Ch7-Idiot; Ch7-Numb]
- Called defenseless or “young lady” → immediate indignation. [Ch7-Azarius-why]
- Barry denying the alliance → ice club if he stood with her; silent hurt if he had already hung back. [Ch7-Wrong-stay; Ch7-Wrong-leave]
- Diane refusing to be saved → will not leave; wants to “beat some sense” into her. [Ch7-Spirits]

### Tendencies
- Assumes betrayal from anyone who looks lessathi or who kept secrets; violence first, collapse when she has to look them in the eye and finish it. [Ch7-Yes; Ch7-Kill]
- Covers fear and guilt with coldness, snark, or calling Barry an idiot—not with staying away. [Ch7-Tap-truth; Ch7-Idiot]
- Protects others by isolating herself (private ambush; “I came alone”). [Ch7-Intro; Ch7-Go-call]
- Rejects pity and labels; Rose’s indifference to the banshee word is what makes her laugh. [Ch7-Sorry-beacon; Ch7-Acquaintances]
- For Diane she will plead, duel a dragon, and refuse retreat. [Ch7-In-or-out; Ch7-Kate-vs-Hadrik; Ch7-Spirits]

### Relationships
- **Barry:** Trusted, then accused, then the one person she believes has been keeping her alive since they met. Friendship is restored on his terms; she still expects to be a danger to him. [Ch7-Yes; Ch7-Knife; Ch7-Numb]
- **Daren:** She reads his “human” gaze (or lack of it) correctly; treats his purification as something he wanted from the start. Snarks, then accepts his healing without warmth. [Ch7-Love; Ch7-Kill; Ch7-Tap-truth]
- **Rose:** Briefly suspected as a recapture accomplice; later the only one whose “you were a mage?” comes without horror. [Ch7-Yes; Ch7-Acquaintances]
- **Diane:** Best friend, rescue target, then the person who calls her an idiot for coming back. Kate cannot process voluntary collaboration with the lessathi. [Ch7-Sorry-beacon; Ch7-Spirits]
- **Hadrik:** Stranger who blocks a mob for her; she is shocked he would stake the Diane lead on Barry, then thanks him and tries to pay. [Ch7-Dwarf-vs-fire; Ch7-In-or-out; Ch7-Kate-vs-Hadrik]
- **Azarius:** Confused by being classed as a woman mage, not a banshee. [Ch7-Woman-mage]

## Character: Rose

### Facts and appearance
- Left at the river when the others are teleported; runs the whole way to the clearing. [Ch7-Okay; Ch7-Smoke]
- Local knowledge: forest spirits, sacred woods, the golden fox. [Ch7-Home-address; Ch7-Allowed]
- Did not ask what “banshee” meant when everyone else used it. [Ch7-Acquaintances]

### History
- On the tap route, gasps at Kate’s ankle and asks who did it. [Ch7-Tap-truth]
- Transceiver at max volume can expose the group; she still comes when called. [Ch7-Watch-not-call; Ch7-Okay]
- Introduces herself to Hadrik and Azarius; is the one who says they are going with Kate. [Ch7-Smoke; Ch7-Ask-me-anything]

### Reactions
- Tremor → “please don’t be a dragon.” [Ch7-Giant]
- Teleport / missing party → panic, then immediate “I’ll be right there.” [Ch7-Okay]
- Reunion → relief first, questions second; handshake for the giant. [Ch7-Smoke]
- Kate’s goodbye bow → treats accompaniment as obvious, citing the shortcuts Kate made possible. [Ch7-Ask-me-anything]
- Hadrik as a dragon → screams until Daren names the shapeshift. [Ch7-Refuse]
- Sacred woods → cut the lecture, move. [Ch7-Spirits]

### Tendencies
- Social glue: courtesy to strangers and to Azarius’s ignored retainer. [Ch7-Smoke; Ch7-Everything]
- Labels bounce off her; she follows people, not categories. [Ch7-Acquaintances]
- Repays escorts with more escorting. [Ch7-Ask-me-anything]
- Dragon-panic is real; forest-spirit caution is practical, not theatrical. [Ch7-Refuse; Ch7-Home-address]

### Relationships
- **Kate:** Warm, non-horrified; insists the group stay with her after the Beacon reveal. [Ch7-Acquaintances; Ch7-Ask-me-anything]
- **Barry / Daren:** Loyalty without taking sides in their Kate fight; transceiver contact is purely practical. [Ch7-Okay; Ch7-Ask-me-anything]
- **Hadrik:** Friendly first meeting; asks why he fights. [Ch7-Smoke; Ch7-Rush]
- **Azarius / Henry:** Polite goodbye that startles Henry. [Ch7-Everything]

## Character: Hadrik

### Facts and appearance
- Introduces himself as Hadrik; friends call him “the dwarf.” About four feet in the form he wears, black beard to the chest, leather armor, jolly booming laugh. [Ch7-Giant; Ch7-Trap]
- Actually a Northern giant; dwarf body is a shapeshift (short legs he is unused to). Can revert to a fifty-foot giant or a black dragon near Tyrath’s size. [Ch7-Accept; Ch7-Refuse]
- Keeps most of his mass/strength when small; no-magic punches crater ground and return a fire beam. [Ch7-Watch-call; Ch7-Dwarf-vs-fire]
- Clan credo: “Mahad selak / Mahad maktar” (glory in life, glory in death). [Ch7-Rush]

### History
- Broadcasts a practice bout to the whole area; spars Eiden under a no-magic rule until Eiden walks off. [Ch7-Giant; Ch7-Wait2]
- That afternoon Diane beat him in one spell, refused her handlers’ order to kill him, and taught him how Beacon mages differ from banshees. [Ch7-Rush]
- Steps between Kate and the mob; later guides the party to Diane’s camp after a deal (Barry’s fall-down spar, or Kate’s magic duel). [Ch7-Help; Ch7-In-or-out; Ch7-Kate-vs-Hadrik]
- Honors the deal, refuses payment, takes a shortcut into sacred woods. [Ch7-Kate-vs-Hadrik; Ch7-Ask-me-anything]

### Reactions
- Strong names (stillwater, dragon-fighter) → loud delight, not caution. [Ch7-Wait2; Ch7-In-or-out]
- A woman being called a monster → plants himself in front, “treat a lady with disrespect,” smirking “Is she, now?” at the banshee claim. [Ch7-Help; Ch7-Azarius-vs-summoner]
- Elf snobbery → ale-in-the-veins / stick-up-the-arse banter. [Ch7-Summoner-vs-dwarf]
- Easy win over Barry → disappointed; clean loss → “well fought,” pays the deal. [Ch7-Accept2]
- Secret exposed → laughs, jokes about blackmail, then drops it when Barry will not report him. [Ch7-Home-address]
- Awkward walking silence → “ask me anything,” then cuts off before a home address. [Ch7-Ask-me-anything; Ch7-Home-address]

### Tendencies
- Fighting is identity (“why does a bird flap its wings?”), inherited from a warrior clan. [Ch7-Rush]
- Chivalry overrides the banshee consensus; he already has an aura test from Diane. [Ch7-Rush; Ch7-Help]
- A deal is a deal: no extra pay, no backing out after a dragon loss. [Ch7-Kate-vs-Hadrik]
- Does not hold back well even in “sparring.” [Ch7-Accept]
- Live-and-let-live with other tournament frauds. [Ch7-Home-address]

### Relationships
- **Eiden:** Eager sparring partner; respects the no-magic rule and the stillwater’s lethality. [Ch7-Wait2; Ch7-Watch-call2]
- **Kate:** Defends her on principle, then as Diane’s friend; startled by how hard she pleads. [Ch7-Help; Ch7-In-or-out]
- **Barry:** Wanted opponent; credits the giant deduction; glances at him when telling Rose he found his challenger. [Ch7-Smoke; Ch7-Home-address]
- **Diane:** Lost to her, lived because she defied the ancients; repeats her Beacon lesson. [Ch7-Rush]
- **Rose:** Easy grin and handshake. [Ch7-Smoke]

## Character: Eiden

### Facts and appearance
- Floats with hands in pockets, eyes closed; “little brat” to Barry. [Ch7-Trap]
- Teleports every nearby contestant into Hadrik’s clearing. [Ch7-Trap]
- Says he is not in the tournament; still accepts a no-magic spar rather than “crush you with a flick.” [Ch7-Wait2]
- Superhuman unarmed speed/power; condenses Felran’s fire into a sphere and returns it. [Ch7-Watch-call; Ch7-Watch-call2]
- Answers Rose by name over the transceiver. [Ch7-Watch-not-call]

### History
- Gathers the field expecting a trap, finds none, calls it disappointing. [Ch7-Trap]
- Starts the Hadrik duel, kills Felran when interrupted, then points the crowd at Barry as “another stillwater” and leaves when bored. [Ch7-Watch-call2; Ch7-Intimidate]

### Reactions
- Called “young stillwater” → mock confusion, claims to be older than Azarius. [Ch7-Wait2]
- Challenge → smiling acceptance, then a rule that keeps it interesting. [Ch7-Wait2]
- Felran’s column of fire → closed-eye calm, one raised eyebrow, instant execution. [Ch7-Watch-call2]
- Barry visible in the crowd → wicked smile; uses the dragon fight as public proof. [Ch7-Watch-call2; Ch7-Who-me]
- No further entertainment → teleport out. [Ch7-Help; Ch7-Intimidate]

### Tendencies
- Spectacle and boredom drive him more than sides or the tournament. [Ch7-Trap; Ch7-Help]
- Amplifies Barry’s stillwater rumor for the crowd. [Ch7-Watch-not-call; Ch7-Watch-call2]
- Casual, smiling cruelty; does not stay to manage the aftermath. [Ch7-Watch-call2; Ch7-Summoner-vs-dwarf]

### Relationships
- **Barry:** Prior acquaintance turned prop; names him stillwater and offers him to Hadrik. [Ch7-Watch-call2]
- **Hadrik:** Brief, almost courteous sparring interest. [Ch7-Wait2]
- **Daren / Kate / Rose:** Knows them well enough to answer Rose and to have dragged the three mages in; Daren reads the gathering as a hope they would kill each other. [Ch7-Watch-not-call; Ch7-Smoke]
