# Character Profiles

These pages summarize recurring patterns for the main companions across all three English books. Extraction notes in [`_segments`](_segments) keep the source trail by chapter range.

- [Barry](barry.md) — First-person protagonist who treats magic as loopholes and prep, jokes while shaken, and will not quit the Magium.
- [Daren](daren.md) — Armored white-mage healer who leads by vow, talks like a legend, then undercuts himself when the save fails.
- [Kate](kate.md) — Guarded ice mage who rations trust, keeps a debt ledger, and spends herself for people she has already claimed.
- [Rose](rose.md) — Local herbalist and peacemaker whose first instinct is to feed, explain, and put herself between harm and someone she loves.
- [Hadrik](hadrik.md) — Northern giant traveling as a dwarf: honor, ale, and reckless strength first, with a hard line against finishing the helpless.
- [Flower](flower.md) — Cheerful ethereal fire mage who shares a body with Illuna, treats a new group as family, and will not stand idle when people are being erased.
- [Illuna](illuna.md) — Separate strategist in that shared body: dry, mission-first, and caring through plans, healing, and vetoes rather than warmth.
- [Arraka](arraka.md) — Amulet-bound banshee whose real help arrives as insults, delayed reveals, and jokes timed to hurt.
- [Eiden](eiden.md) — Recurring stillwater who keeps deals, withholds answers for amusement, and watches whether anyone can prove him wrong.
- [Leila](leila.md) — Mute lessathi companion who writes in midair, minimizes herself off the field, and is already moving once she has a read.
- [Melindra](melindra.md) — Stillwater air mage who says she does not need friends; teasing is the cover, and loyalty shows up in crises and teaching.
